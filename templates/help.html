Help

Chatting with KNIGHT

Type your question in the box at the bottom and press Enter. KNIGHT answers about enrollment, offices, teachers, rooms, fees, and school processes. You can switch the model from the dropdown above the input, or use the suggestion chips to start quickly.

Saving your chats

You can chat right away without an account. If you want your sessions saved and available the next time you visit, use Log in at the lower-left of the sidebar. Chats you made before logging in are moved into your account automatically.

Light / dark mode

Use the Light mode / Dark mode button in the sidebar. Your choice is remembered in this browser.

Reporting a problem

Wrong answer, error, or login issue? Open the menu at the top of the sidebar, choose Report a Problem, pick a category, and describe what happened. The admin receives it in the Tickets module of the dashboard.
