# KCC History - Campus Expansion and Carmelite Sisters (1996)
Campus Expansion - St. Columban Arts Center & Annex: An additional phase of the college's physical structure is the St. Columban Arts Center, the socio-cultural venue not only for students but also for the community. Behind it is the annex, a concrete building housing the new college canteen and textbook center on the ground floor and the College library on the upper floor.
1996 - Carmelite Sisters Invited: In 1996, the Carmelite Sisters of Our Lady (Carm, O.L.) were invited by Most Rev. Vicente Navarra, D.D., Bishop of Kabankalan and Chairman of the Board of Trustees, to assist Msgr. Rogelio B. Cruz, the School President, in the management of the school.
