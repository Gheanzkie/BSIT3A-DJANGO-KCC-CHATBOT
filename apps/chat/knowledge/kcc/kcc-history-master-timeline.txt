KCC History (Kasaysayan) - Complete Timeline 1927-2025, All Milestones
Ang kasaysayan ng KCC (kumpletong timeline, 1927-2025):
1927 - Founded as Kabankalan Institute (later Kabankalan Private Academy).
1940 - First commencement exercises. 1941-1944 classes suspended (World War II); resumed SY 1945-1946.
1953 - Columban Fathers (Fr. Thomas J. Cronin) acquire Kabankalan Academy.
1963-1964 - College and Elementary departments open; St. Paul Sisters assist.
1983 - Diocesan administration begins (Msgr. Josefino I. Iledan; Presentation Sisters assist).
1987 - Great fire razes high school building, parish convent, Old Catholic church; reconstruction follows.
1988 - Kabankalan becomes a new Diocese (First Bishop Most Rev. Vicente M. Navarra); Good Shepherd Sisters serve a year.
Campus expansion: St. Columban Arts Center and annex (canteen, library).
1996 - Carmelite Sisters of Our Lady invited to assist school management.
2003-2011 - Bishop Patricio A. Buzon era; 2009 KCC-Technological Center (Barangay Talubangi); 2011 Fr. Radny T. Bayog appointed president.
2018-2019 - Bishop Louie P. Galbines; Fr. Eugene D. Lucerna II appointed president - first alumnus (HS Batch 1996).
2025 - Fr. Richard L. Lachica, MAEd appointed school president.
