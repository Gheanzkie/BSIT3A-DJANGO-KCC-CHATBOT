# KCC High School Tuition and School Fees - Overview (AY 2026-2027)

Answer guide for tuition, school fees, matriculation, "magkano ang tuition", "bayad sa
school" and "matrikula" questions about the High School Department (Grades 7 to 12) of
Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. (KCC), Academic Year 2026-2027. These are High School
fees only - never use them for the Elementary or College departments.

Also asked as: tuition fee list, how much is the tuition, school fees 2026-2027, high school fees, bayad sa school, matrikula, PHP tuition KCC.

## How to answer

1. Give the total first, then the item-by-item breakdown when the user asks for details.
2. Grades 7, 8 and 9 share one schedule: ₱16,005.00.
3. For Grade 11 and Grade 12 ask whether the student came from a public or a private
   school, because the FAPE/QVR deduction changes the total. If unknown, give both totals.
4. Reproduce the published totals exactly. Do not recalculate, round or adjust them.
5. Never invent fees, discounts, payment schedules, installment plans or extra charges.
6. State that these are High School Department fees for Academic Year 2026-2027.
7. If a detail is not in these notes, refer the user to the KCC Registrar's Office or
   Accounting Office.
