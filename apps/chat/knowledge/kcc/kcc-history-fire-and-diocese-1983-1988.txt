KCC History - Diocesan Administration, the Great Fire, and the New Diocese (1983-1988)
1983 - Diocesan Administration Begins: The College department was built on a hectare of land donated by the Zayco family. In 1983 the management services of the Sisters of St. Paul terminated and the school administration shifted to Diocesan priests headed by Msgr. Josefino I. Iledan as President, assisted by the Presentation Sisters.
1987 - The Great Fire & Reconstruction: On March 14, 1987, while the town celebrated its 80th Foundation Day, a fire of dubious origin razed the high school building, the parish convent, and the Old Catholic church. Massive rehabilitation and reconstruction followed; the high school department was temporarily relocated to the College campus while a permanent three-storey building was built on the newly acquired lot adjacent to the College.
1988 - New Diocese: Kabankalan was made a new Diocese with Most Reverend Vicente M. Navarra, D.D. as its First Bishop. The College obtained the services of the Religious of the Good Shepherd who served for a year.
