Enrollment for School Year 2025-2026 Now Open
Date: March 2025 | Category: Enrollment
Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. officially opens its enrollment for School Year 2025-2026. All departments are accepting new and returning students:
- Elementary Department (Kindergarten to Grade 6)
- Junior High School (Grades 7-10)
- Senior High School (Grades 11-12) - Academic & TVL Tracks
- College Department - BSBA, BSIT, BEEd, BSEd, BSN, BS Criminology
Interested students may visit the Registrar's Office or contact the admissions team for enrollment details and requirements. Scholarship programs and financial assistance are also available for qualifying students.
Enroll now and be part of the KCC community!
