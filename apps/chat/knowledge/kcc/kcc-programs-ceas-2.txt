KCC College Programs - Education, Arts and Sciences (Part 2)
Ang mga sumusunod ay college courses/kurso (degree programs) ng KCC sa ilalim ng College of Education, Arts and Sciences (CEAS) - Part 2.
Bachelor of Physical Education (BPEd) - Careers: PE Teacher, Sports Coach, Fitness Instructor, Athletic Director, Sports Development Officer. Develops competent physical education teachers and sports professionals, with knowledge in exercise science, sports coaching, and health-related fitness instruction.
Bachelor of Arts in Philosophy (AB Philosophy) - Careers: Philosophy Professor, Seminary Studies, Ethics Consultant, Writer/Journalist, Law School Preparation. Develops critical thinking, analytical reasoning, and ethical reflection; students explore fundamental questions about existence, knowledge, values, and the human condition.
BS in Psychology - Careers: Psychologist, Guidance Counselor, Human Resource Officer, Behavioral Therapist, Research Analyst. Provides a comprehensive understanding of human behavior, mental processes, and psychological well-being, with skills in counseling, assessment, and research.
