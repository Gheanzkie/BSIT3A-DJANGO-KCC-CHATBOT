KCC Campus Life - Sports and Athletics (KCC Knights)
District Sports Meet 2025: Opening Program held at the St. Columban Arts Center, Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc.
KADICOSCA 2026 Knights: "Charging forward" - KCC Knights stand strong for District 2 at the City Sports Meet, December 10-13.
KADICOSCA '26 Basketball Glory: The KCC Men's Basketball team reclaimed the championship title, dethroning the defending champions, the BCC Stewards, 75-69. Trailing 25-13 and 38-32 in the first half, the Knights mounted a comeback to lead 57-50 by the third quarter and sealed the game in the final quarter. The feat marks the Knights' determination to bring the title back to their alma mater. Congratulations, KCC Knights!
