# KCC History - Current School President (2025)
On April 15, 2025, Bishop Louie P. Galbines, SThD, D.D. appointed Rev. Fr. Richard L. Lachica, MAEd as the new school president of Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc., effective Academic Year 2025-2026.
Fr. Lachica succeeded Rev. Fr. Eugene D. Lucerna II, PhD, RPsy, RGC, who served as school president since 2019.
