KCC Student Handbook Appendix D: AI Guidelines — Academic Integrity (Part 1 of 2)
The mere utilization of AI and other technological resources does not inherently obstruct learning; rather, it hinges on employing these tools while upholding academic integrity. AI-generated content may lack accuracy or clarity, potentially leading to misrepresented research, uncredited sources, and hindering students' comprehension of the subject matter, thus undermining the educational process.
