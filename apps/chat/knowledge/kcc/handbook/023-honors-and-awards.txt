KCC Student Handbook: Honors and Awards — Section 2.10
Selection of honor students in any grade level will be based on DepEd Order No. 36, s. 2016 (unless revoked by a new issuance of DepEd order). The award for Academic Excellence is given to learners from Kindergarten to Grade 12 who have attained an average of at least 90 and with a cut-off grade of 85 in all learning areas in all quarters, and will be given due recognition by the school at the end of every school year.

Students who excelled in co-curricular and extra-curricular activities will be given due award/recognition at the end of every school year as determined by the school committee.

A loyalty award will be given to any graduating student who has a continuous tenure from nursery to Grade 12.
