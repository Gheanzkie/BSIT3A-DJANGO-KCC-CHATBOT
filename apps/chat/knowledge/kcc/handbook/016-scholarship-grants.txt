KCC Student Handbook: Scholarship Grants, Requirements and Privileges — Section 2.3
2.3.1 Academic Scholarship — A. Entrance Scholarship.

For Elementary to Senior High School, Rank 1 — 100% free tuition fees for the whole school year, provided that he/she meets the following requirements: the class from which he/she graduated is not less than 50 students; submits a certificate of honors from the school where he/she graduated stating his/her rank.

For Elementary to Senior High School, Rank 2 — 50% discount on tuition fees for the whole school year, provided he/she meets the following requirements: the class from which he/she graduated is not less than 50 students; submits a certificate of honors from the school where he/she graduated stating his/her rank.

Note: Continuation of the scholarship will depend upon the student's rank in the ranking list at the end of the semester.
