KCC Student Handbook Appendix C: Types of Child Neglect
Child Neglect consists of actions where there is failure to appropriately meet the care needs of a child. There are five types of child neglect. Children are often exposed to multiple forms of neglect. Neglect can occur through the actual occurrence of neglect or through the threat to neglect a child.
1. Physical Neglect — a failure to respond to a child's physical care needs for food, shelter, clothing, and medical care.
2. Psychological Neglect — a failure to contribute towards the development of a child's sense of identity, worth and capacity.
3. Emotional Neglect — a failure to facilitate the development of secure emotional attachment between a child and others, through the failure to provide a child with love, nurturance and care.
4. Cognitive Neglect — a failure to contribute towards a child's learning of culturally and socially appropriate knowledge and skills.
5. Moral Neglect — a failure to develop a child's capacity to understand and enact good and acceptable personal behaviors and conduct.

When a child is maltreated, it is common that a child is both abused and neglected.
