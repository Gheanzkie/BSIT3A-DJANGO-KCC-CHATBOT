KCC Student Handbook: School Uniform, Haircut and Grooming Policy — Section 3.5
The prescribed school uniform must be worn from Mondays to Fridays.

HAIR CUT POLICY — Students must observe the following:
a. Neat and well-groomed hair. Allowed haircut is only barber's cut with no lines or fashionable haircut designs.
b. No wearing of multiple earrings.
c. No wearing of earrings for male students.
d. No hair dye exceeding color spectrum 6.
e. No cross-dressing.
f. Having tattoos is strongly discouraged.
