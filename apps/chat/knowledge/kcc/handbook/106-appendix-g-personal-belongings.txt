KCC Student Handbook Appendix G: Personal Belongings — Part 3 of 3
1. Personal Responsibility: Students are responsible for their personal belongings and should organize them in order to minimize clutter in the classroom. In the event where students have to leave the classroom in order to do performance tasks, attend Mass and prayer service, and other official school activities, students must bring their personal belongings such as wallets, laptops, tablets, cellphones, and other gadgets with them.
2. Lost and Found: The Office of the Discipline Officer is in charge of all 'Lost and Found' items. Students must check this office if they misplace any belongings.

Consequences for Non-Compliance: Failure to comply with the guidelines on campus and classroom cleanliness may result in appropriate disciplinary actions as per the school's existing policies outlined in the Student Daily Guide (Summary of Offences and Maximum Penalties). Vandalism, removal of wall paint due to sticking of tapes, or destruction of any school property shall be charged accordingly.
