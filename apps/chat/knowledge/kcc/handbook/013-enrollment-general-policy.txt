KCC Student Handbook: Enrollment — General Policy — Section 2
Enrollment at Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. is a privilege. Therefore, the School Administration reserves the right to admit students/pupils on the basis of moral character, academic standing and other credentials.

Any student/pupil admitted to the school is subject to the regulations of the school as set out in this Daily Guide and in other appropriate government and school legislation. Although Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. is a Catholic school, it is happy to enroll students of other religious beliefs, provided they will follow its rules and regulations.

The enrollment of students/pupils for High School/Elementary will normally be conducted during the registration days as indicated in the school calendar and in the school information boards. Enrollment at other times can be arranged through the School Registrar/Principal. Students/Pupils must make a personal appearance for the enrollment procedures.
