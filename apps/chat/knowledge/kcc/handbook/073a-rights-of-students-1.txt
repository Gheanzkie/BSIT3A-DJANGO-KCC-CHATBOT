KCC Student Handbook: Rights of the Students (Part 1 of 2) — Section 8.1
The rights of the student are subject to the limitations prescribed by law and the school policies and regulations. The rights of the students shall be:
1. To receive proper and satisfactory instructions in the course he/she enrolled in, in accordance with the approved educational objectives and standards of the school.
2. To be respected in his/her rights, and to be fairly treated as a student and as a person consistent with human dignity.
3. To form, join or lead in such student organizations or associations as may be recognized or authorized to operate by the school.
4. To avail of the use of school facilities for his curricular activities as may be authorized by the school.
