KCC Student Handbook Appendix I: Student's/Pupil's Agreement with KCC (Part 1 of 3)
I hereby pledge to abide by the following disciplinary norms hereunder stated as fundamental guidelines for the attainment of a sound Christian education. As a Student/Pupil:
1. I fully accept the school rules and other directives set by the school authorities, knowing that said rules and directives are for my own good.
2. I shall cooperate to the best of my ability in order to attain the aim of the school, which is to form in me a practicing Catholic and a concerned Filipino citizen.
3. Once enrolled in Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. - Kabankalan City, I shall uphold the teachings of the school within and outside the campus.
