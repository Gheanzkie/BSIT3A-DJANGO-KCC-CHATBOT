KCC Student Handbook Appendix J: Parents Agreement with KCC (Part 3 of 3) — Terms 6 to 10
6. We shall attend the Parent's Assembly every grading period as well as other meetings called by the school in order to share our views and sentiments towards a better implementation of the school's objectives.
7. We shall confer with teachers and school by personally affixing our signatures to the Report Card after every grading period.
8. We shall follow up our child's study habits at home. We shall support our child's extracurricular activities in school undertaken with our permission.
9. We recognize the Parent's Assembly as the forum for setting disputes that arise regarding school affairs not covered by the rules and policies of the school, without prejudice to our right to consult the matter with the DepEd for enlightenment.
10. We retain our rights to complain and air grievances only during the Parent's Assembly and not through other forums, without any way harming the name of the school, its administration and faculty, nor disrupting or hampering school activities through pickets or demonstrations.
