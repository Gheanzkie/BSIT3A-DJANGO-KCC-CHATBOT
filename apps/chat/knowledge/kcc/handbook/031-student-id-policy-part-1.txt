KCC Student Handbook: Student ID Policy — No ID No Entry (Part 1 of 2) — Section 3.5
Students are issued IDs at the beginning of the school year. No ID, No Entry policy is observed in the campus. The ID card must be worn prior to the student's entrance to the campus. It may be clipped, pinned or worn with string and visibly displayed on the front of the body with the student's picture facing front. It must also be worn in other school-related activities outside the school.

The ID is strictly non-transferable. The ID must not be altered or mutilated.
