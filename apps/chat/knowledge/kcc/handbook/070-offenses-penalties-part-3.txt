KCC Student Handbook: Summary of Offenses and Maximum Penalties (Part 3 of 4)
Penalties are listed as: 1st offense; 2nd offense; 3rd offense. (** = penalty is determined by the circumstances and gravity of the offense.)

- Misuse of I.D. card: C; D; E
- No excuse slip after / Tardiness: A; B; C
- No ID / Not wearing of ID: A; B; C
- Possession of Deadly Weapons (**): E; F; G
- Possession / ownership of Firecrackers, exploring firecrackers causing danger of injury: C; D; E
- Possession / ownership of indecent / pornographic materials (**): C; D; E
- Smoking or possession of cigarette and/or any smoking paraphernalia: C; D; E
- Tampering of Notices, ID Cards, Report/Conduct cards, Lockers of other students, etc. (**): D; E; F
- Theft: E; F; G
- Truancy: B; C; D
- Vandalism (**): A*; B; C (A* with reparation)
