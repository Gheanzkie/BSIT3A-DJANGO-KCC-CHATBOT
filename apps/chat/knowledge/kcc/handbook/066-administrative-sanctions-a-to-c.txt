KCC Student Handbook: Administrative Sanctions — Reprimand, Post, Suspension (7.3 A-C)
A. REPRIMAND / WARNING — is a written or oral reproach addressed to an erring student.

B. POST — is a penalty imposed by the Discipline Officer for serious violations of school rules and classroom discipline. There are 1 or 2-hour posts depending on the seriousness of the offense committed. It consists of community service (cleaning the surroundings and school facilities before and after the class) designed and supervised by the Committee on Discipline.

C. SUSPENSION — is a penalty in which the school is allowed to deny or deprive an erring student's attendance in class for a period not exceeding 20% of the prescribed class days for the school year.
