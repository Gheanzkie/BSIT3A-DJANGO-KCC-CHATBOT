KCC Student Handbook: Summary of Offenses — School's Reserved Rights
The School reserves the right to place on probation, to suspend or dismiss any student who fails to give satisfactory evidence of earnestness of purpose and active cooperation in all regulations and conduct and proper behavior.

The School also reserves the right to likewise penalize students who engage in practices or activities which are contrary to the goals, objectives and philosophy of Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc.

A student's liability shall not be confined to the enumeration as contained in the school rules, since some may be generally worded to encompass a number of related wrongdoings. (Reference: revised MRPS)
