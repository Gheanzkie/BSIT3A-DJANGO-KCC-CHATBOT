KCC Student Handbook Appendix C: Types of Child Abuse (Part 1 of 2)
1. Physical Abuse — occurs when a child's body is physically injured. This abuse may occur through punching, throwing, slapping, shaking, strangling, kicking, poisoning, drowning or suffocating (U.K. Dept of Health, 1999, p5).
2. Psychological Abuse — is sustained, repetitive, inappropriate behavior which damages, or substantially reduces a child's sense of self-identity, worth and efficacy, and therefore impacts upon the child's creative and developmental potential of crucially important mental faculties and mental processes — these include intelligence, memory, perception, attention, language and moral development (Meemeduma, 2005; extending O'Hagan's (2002) definition).
