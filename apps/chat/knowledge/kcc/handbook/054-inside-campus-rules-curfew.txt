KCC Student Handbook: Conduct Inside the Campus and Curfew — Section 5.4
The school is an environment-friendly institution; hence, cleanliness and orderliness must be observed. Pay respect to classmates, schoolmates, teachers and all school authorities by avoiding unnecessary noise that may cause annoyance and distractions. Students are to refrain from sitting in parapets or windowsills and stairs. Moreover, students are not allowed to wear caps inside the school, office, in the chapel and along the corridor. Students should not stay inside the school campus beyond curfew hours unless they are engaged in school activities with permits granted by the principal.

Curfew Time: Curfew time is at 6:00 P.M. during class days.
