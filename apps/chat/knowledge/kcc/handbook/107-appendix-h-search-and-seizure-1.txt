KCC Student Handbook Appendix H: Stakeholders' Right Against Unreasonable Searches and Seizures — Part 1 of 2
If the occasion warrants, authorized personnel of the school can validly conduct searches and seizures of stakeholders. Except for the following instances, no searches and seizures shall be deemed valid:
a. Searches made at the point of entrance and exit in the campus and in the buildings of the school by authorized personnel of the school.
b. Searches and seizures of the illegal articles as defined by the Revised Penal Code and other related penal laws, Student Daily Guide and Administrative and Employee Manual.
c. Searches and seizures of articles by duly authorized personnel (open to his/her naked eye and hand) as evidence of a crime, contraband, or otherwise subject to seizure.
d. Searches made when any stakeholder is attempting to commit, is committing, has just committed, or has been committing a crime or a serious infraction of the school's rules and regulations.
e. Searches made with a valid search warrant.
f. Searches under any circumstance conducted on reasonable grounds.
