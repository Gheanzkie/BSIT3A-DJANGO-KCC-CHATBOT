KCC Student Handbook: School Philosophy (Part 2 of 2) — Section 1.1
As an educational institution, KCC seeks academic excellence with a strong moral foundation characterized by integrity, compassion, and generosity. Our students and graduates are equipped with the skills, knowledge, and disposition necessary to contribute positively to society and to respond effectively to the needs of the Diocese, the nation, and the global community.

KCC aims to achieve all these through the delivery of exceptional instruction; the development of suitable learning spaces and facilities; the conduct of innovative research and creative projects; and the engagement in strong and purposeful outreach.
