KCC Student Handbook: Clubs — Membership Rules — Section 4.2
A student/pupil may belong to no more than two (2) clubs. Membership in more than the advised number is strongly discouraged, for this may adversely affect his/her academic performance.

A student who is active in school activities is expected to maintain a good scholastic record. If these activities seem to be the cause of poor academic performance, he/she will be required to cease his/her participation.

All clubs/councils/organizations are required to submit to the SSAO or CMO coordinators the list of members and officers, constitution and by-laws, and plan of action for the school year for endorsement and approval of the principal before the induction ceremony, so that they will be recognized as official clubs/councils and organizations of the school.
