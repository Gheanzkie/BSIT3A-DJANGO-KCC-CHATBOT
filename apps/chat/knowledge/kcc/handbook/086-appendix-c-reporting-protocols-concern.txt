KCC Student Handbook Appendix C: Reporting Protocols — Maltreatment Concern (Faculty and Staff)
Responsibility of Faculty and Staff in reporting a maltreatment concern. The following are the steps to be followed:
- The person with the initial concern identifies in his/her mind answers to the following questions: a. 'Which of the seven care areas am I concerned about?' b. 'What did I see, hear, touch and smell to be concerned about?'
- He/she writes down notes in response to the questions (including time/date, names, location).
- He/she decides whether he/she is concerned enough to inform school management.
- The person with the initial concern reports to management (School Principal or The School Director).
- If the School Principal/School Director determines the concern has sufficient substance, he/she will, without delay, inform the DDMA.
- The Diocesan Director of Ministerial Accountability (DDMA) will direct the Diocesan Contact Person (DCP) to conduct investigation. Complaints may be referred directly to the DCP or the DDMA if there is sufficient reason to bypass the School Director, but the normal path should otherwise be followed.
