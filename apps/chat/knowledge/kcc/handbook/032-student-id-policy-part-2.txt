KCC Student Handbook: Student ID Policy — Confiscation and Lost ID (Part 2 of 2) — Section 3.5
ID not worn in the school campus will be confiscated by any teacher or staff and must be turned over to the Discipline Officer. All confiscated IDs must be claimed at the Discipline Office not later than 48 hours after confiscation.

Lost ID should be replaced immediately. It should be reported to the adviser for issuance of a temporary pass, and the following requirements must be complied with and submitted to the VPSM:
- Affidavit of loss
- Conference with the adviser
- Replacement fee
