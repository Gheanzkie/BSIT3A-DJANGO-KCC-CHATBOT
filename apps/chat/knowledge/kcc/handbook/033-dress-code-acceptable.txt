KCC Student Handbook: Dress Code for Civilian Attire — Acceptable Clothing — Section 3.6
During school activities, students may be allowed to wear civilian attire provided that it conforms to the following. The following are ACCEPTABLE:
- Blouses with sleeves, t-shirts, polo shirts, tops covering the midriffs when standing naturally
- Strapless and sleeveless tops may be worn with a cardigan/sweater that fully covers all areas
- Skirts, dresses and shorts must be knee-length or lower
- Mid-thigh or lower athletic shorts (allowed only during sports competitions)
- Jeans, pants, jeans with holes, shred or rips at knee level or lower
- Sandals, step-in, shoes
