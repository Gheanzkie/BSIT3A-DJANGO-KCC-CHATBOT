KCC Student Handbook: Conduct in the Workshop, Computer Room and TLE Room — Section 5.3
The workshop, computer room and TLE room are places solely for work and hands-on activities. Always observe silence. Take proper care of the tools, computer units and paraphernalia. Each student has his/her own materials ready and uses them for his/her activities when required. Return all borrowed tools to the subject teacher or to the property in-charge. Clean the workshop, computer room and TLE room after using and before leaving it. Eating is strictly prohibited in these areas.
