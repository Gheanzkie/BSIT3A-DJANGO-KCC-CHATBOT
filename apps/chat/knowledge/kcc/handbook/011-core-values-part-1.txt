KCC Student Handbook: Core Values of a KCCian — K, C, C, I (Part 1 of 2) — Section 1.4
KCCIANS, always remember who you are.

K — KINDNESS: A KCCian upholds a deep sense of unity and empathy, embodying shared purpose and mutual support within the school and wider community.

C — CHRIST-CENTEREDNESS: A KCCian is grounded in the living faith in God, guided by Gospel values that inspire words and actions in everyday life.

C — CREATIVITY: A KCCian embraces innovation in the pursuit of new ideas through research and creative problem-solving for the service of Church and society.

I — INTEGRITY: A KCCian is committed to moral responsibility and academic honesty, exemplifying Gospel-rooted principles in academic works, personal choices, and leadership.
