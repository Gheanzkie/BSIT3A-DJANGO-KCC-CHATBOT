KCC Student Handbook Appendix F: Campus-Wide Prayer Schedule (Part 3 of 3)
At 12:00 P.M., 3:00 P.M. and 6:00 P.M., our school's public address (PA) system will play the recorded prayers. Throughout the entire campus (offices, classrooms, libraries, laboratories, canteen, gym, lobby, gazebos, security post, and playground), every KCCian must stand in attention, head bowed, right hand over the chest, and join in praying the Angelus, Regina Caeli (during Easter season), and 3 o'clock Prayer.

These prayers interrupt whatever task KCCians are in, just as the arrival of Christ in the Incarnation interrupts the world. Far from being a distraction, the welcome interruption of these prayers teaches a life-long lesson: no activity is more important than prayer — not work, not meetings, not study, not conversation, not extracurricular activities, not even recess.
