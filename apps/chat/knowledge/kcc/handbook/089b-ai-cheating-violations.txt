KCC Student Handbook Appendix D: AI Guidelines — Cheating and Violations (Part 2 of 2)
Cheating constitutes a grave offense. It detrimentally impacts the academic community by eroding academic integrity, fostering mistrust, and perpetuating unfair competition. Kabankalan Catholic College (KCC) takes a firm stance against cheating and imposes penalties as stated in the Student Daily Guide (Summary of Offences and Maximum Penalties). Violations encompass a range of actions such as cheating on exams, plagiarism, unauthorized reuse of assignments, improper use of electronic devices and the internet, unauthorized collaboration, tampering with graded assignments, forgery, dishonesty, facilitating academic misconduct, and engaging in unfair competition. Lack of awareness of these regulations will not be accepted as an excuse.
