KCC Student Handbook: Admission Policies — Section 2.1
Admission to the Kinder, Grade 7 and Grade 11 is open to all on a first come, first served basis. Test and oral interview will be given before the enrollment. Admission of transferees with no failing grades in any subject from Grades 1 to 12 will also be on a first come first served basis, provided there are vacant slots.

An old student with two or more failures in the final grades will not be accepted unless he or she presents a certificate of attendance in a summer class with passing grades for Grade 8 to 12.

A student under probation who has not shown improvement will not be admitted.
