KCC Student Handbook Appendix I: Student's/Pupil's Agreement with KCC (Part 2 of 3) — School Requirements
4. I recognize that the school requirements include among others: a. Punctuality in reporting in school and class activities. b. Always wearing of the prescribed uniform (neat and clean). c. To observe the prescribed KCCian haircut and refrain from sporting a faddish haircut, not to have my ears pierced or to wear earrings, and to not have tattoos. They are not appropriate for a Christian, as well as for the good name of the school. I shall avoid excessive expressions of effeminacy in words and actions. d. To observe the following things which are considered important school requirements, namely: ID Card, Library Card, Campus Pass, Textbooks and Workbooks, Birth Certificate, Baptismal Certificate, Confirmation Certificate, Parents' Marriage Contract, and other subject requirements.
