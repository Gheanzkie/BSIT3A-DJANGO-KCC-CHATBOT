KCC Student Handbook: Summary of Offenses and Maximum Penalties — Legend and Notes
Penalty letter legend: A = Warning/Reprimand; B = Post/Community Service; C = 1-day Suspension; D = 2-Day Suspension; E = 3-Day Suspension; F = 5-Day Suspension with Disciplinary Probation; G = Dismissal (see the Administrative Sanctions files for full definitions).

Notes: * = with reparation. ** = penalty is determined by the circumstances and gravity of the offense.

N.B. When the offense has a maximum penalty, the Committee on Discipline has the authority to lower down the sanction based on the gravity of the offense. One degree means two days suspension to one day suspension, to two-hour post, to two post and one hour jug.
