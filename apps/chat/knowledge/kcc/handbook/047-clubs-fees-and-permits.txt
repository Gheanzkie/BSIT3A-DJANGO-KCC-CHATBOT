KCC Student Handbook: Clubs — Fees, Financial Statements and Activity Permits — Section 4.2
Clubs may collect membership fees approved by the Principal and VP for Student Ministry. The amount collected from the membership should be turned over to the cashier's office. All clubs are required to submit financial statements at the end of the school year. This should be signed by the club president, the treasurer and the supervising adviser.

No students' organization may impose upon its members any fine, penalty or punishment of any kind except those provided for in its constitution and by-laws.

All activities undertaken by the recognized club should have the permit from the administration at least one week before the schedule.

Any damage on the materials, equipment borrowed will be charged to the person or club concerned. The cleanliness of the venue used should be maintained during and after the activity.
