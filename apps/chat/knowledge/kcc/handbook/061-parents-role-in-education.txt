KCC Student Handbook: Parents' and Guardians' Support — Role in Education — Section 6
The work of education for all the students/pupils of Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. requires the active cooperation of the parents/guardians of the students/pupils.

Daily concern for the academic progress of students/pupils is an important duty of parents/guardians. This includes general interest in their daily learning at school as well as helping them attend to out-of-class activities such as homework, subject research, acquiring correct textbooks, etc.

During the school year, there are many activities involving the students/pupils to which parents/guardians and family are cordially invited. These include a range of religious, cultural and sporting events. Students/pupils certainly derive great encouragement from the presence of the parents and friends on such occasions.
