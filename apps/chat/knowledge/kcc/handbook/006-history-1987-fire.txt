KCC Student Handbook: History of KCC — The 1987 Fire and Rehabilitation — Section 1.2
On March 14, 1987, while the town was celebrating its 80th Foundation Day, a fire of dubious origin razed the high school building, the parish convent and the Old Catholic church to the ground. The catastrophe brought untold financial losses to the school. Massive rehabilitation and reconstruction were immediately instituted as the school faced the opening of the next school year. Meanwhile the high school department was temporarily relocated to the College campus while the permanent three-storey building on the newly acquired lot adjacent to the College was under construction.
