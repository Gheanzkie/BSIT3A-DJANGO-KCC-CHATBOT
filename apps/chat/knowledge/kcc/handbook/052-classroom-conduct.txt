KCC Student Handbook: Conduct in the Classroom and During Classes — Section 5.2
Classrooms should be kept clean and orderly at all times. Every student should always be ready with the necessary materials needed for every classroom activity. Students should respect the rights and properties of their classmates and teachers. Acts of vandalism are strictly prohibited. Silence should be observed during or in between classes and in the absence of the teacher. Avoid boisterous laughter, shouting, whistling and other unnecessary noise. Eating is not allowed during class hours.

Pay respect to the teachers, superiors or visitors who enter or leave the classroom. Every student should be participative in all classroom activities.
