KCC Student Handbook Appendix A: Republic Act No. 9165 — Reporting Duty (Part 2 of 2)
Any teacher or school employee who discovers or finds that any person in the school or within its immediate vicinity is liable for violating any of the said provisions shall have the duty to report the same to the school head or immediate superior, who shall, in turn, report the matter to the proper authorities. Failure to do so, in either case, within a reasonable period from the time of discovery of the violation shall, after due hearing, constitute sufficient cause for disciplinary action by the school authorities.
