KCC Student Handbook Appendix D: Generative AI Acceptable Use Scale — Level 4
Level 4 — Full AI Use with Human Oversight: You may use AI throughout your assessment to support your own work in any way you deem necessary. AI should be a 'copilot' to enhance human creativity. You are responsible for providing human oversight and evaluation of all AI-generated content. All AI-created content must be cited using appropriate formatting and citation style. Link(s) to AI chat(s) must be submitted with the final submission.
