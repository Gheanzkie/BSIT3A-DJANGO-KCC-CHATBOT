KCC Student Handbook: Summary of Offenses and Maximum Penalties (Part 1 of 4)
Penalties are listed as: 1st offense; 2nd offense; 3rd offense. Penalty letters A-G are defined in the Administrative Sanctions files. (** = penalty is determined by the circumstances and gravity of the offense.)

- Academic Dishonesty / Cheating: A; E; F
- Distribution of leakage: E; F; G
- Bringing or possessing alcoholic beverage in the KCC premises, extension facilities, or on school activities: E; F; G
- Class cut: E; F; G
- Classroom Misbehavior: A; B; C
- Coming to school or entering the school premises and its extension while under the influence of alcohol: E; F; G
- Conduct unbecoming of a Christian (**): E; F; G
- Cyberbullying (**): E; F; G
- Defiance of school authorities or rules: E; F; G
- Destroying of personal property of other students: C; E; F
- Disruption of Classes: A; C; D
- Entering classes without admit-to-class slip: A; B; C
- Failure to perform assigned task: A; B; C
- Failure to return report card: A; B; C
- Fighting / Physical Assault (**): E; F; G
- Forgery (**): E; F; G
