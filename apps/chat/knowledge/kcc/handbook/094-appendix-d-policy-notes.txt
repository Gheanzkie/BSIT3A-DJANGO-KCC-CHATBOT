KCC Student Handbook Appendix D: AI Policy — Acknowledgment and Revisions
This policy acknowledges that: it relies on goodwill, fairness, and integrity; instructors may opt for stricter constraints on AI tool usage, ensuring transparency and fairness in grading; and disparities in accessibility to subscription versions of AI tools may necessitate revisions to the policy as distinctions between subscription and free versions become clearer. The policy may undergo revisions in response to other policies and emerging technological advancements in AI tools.
