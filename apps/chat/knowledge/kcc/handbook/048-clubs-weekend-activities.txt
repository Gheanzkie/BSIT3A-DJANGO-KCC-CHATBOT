KCC Student Handbook: Clubs — Weekend Practices and Rehearsals — Section 4.2
For weekend practices/rehearsal/activities, except Sunday, a written letter from the adviser(s) should be made and submitted to the office of the principal for approval, indicating the names of the students who will be involved, with a copy furnished to the guard on duty. Concerned students must bring their ID, waiver or parent's consent signed by their parents/guardians.
