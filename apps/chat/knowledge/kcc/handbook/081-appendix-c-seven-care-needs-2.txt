KCC Student Handbook Appendix C: The Seven Care Needs of Children (Part 2 of 2)
5. Social Care — the need for a child to belong to valued groups within society, to ensure a sense of social inclusion, social giving and social identity.
6. Spiritual Care — the need for a child to develop in an environment where God's presence is considered normal and accepted, to understand the sense of the sacred and a presence personally within the child's own self as well as others; to express worship and prayer; and to sense personal unity with the natural environment, the sustainability of which is valued by the child.
7. Moral Care — the need for a child to learn and understand what is considered 'good' individual and social behavior, to enable the development of a social community of engagement and compassion.

The school is committed to ensuring that its services actually facilitate the meeting of children's seven care needs, the benchmark of a service care environment for schools in the Diocese. The benchmark provides the school faculty and staff with a means to assess and determine when a child may be harmed or be at risk of harm.
