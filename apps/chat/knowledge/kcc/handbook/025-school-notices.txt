KCC Student Handbook: School Notices — Section 2.12
School notices are posted on various bulletin boards and the school's official social media accounts. It is the student's/pupil's responsibility to read these notices. Bulletin boards and the school's official social media accounts are for official use only. Tampering with any of them is a serious offense.

Only with the Principal's permission may a student/pupil post notices or posters on the school's notice boards.
