KCC Student Handbook: History of KCC — Recent Presidents (2003 to present) — Section 1.2
Most Reverend Patricio A. Buzon, SDB, D.D., was installed as the second Bishop of the Diocese of Kabankalan in 2003. In 2005, he administered the School as the Chairman of the Board of Trustees and as School President. Under his management, the KCC-Technological Center situated in Barangay Talubangi, Kabankalan City was established in 2009. In 2011, Rev. Fr. Radny T. Bayog, PhD, was appointed as School President of KCC.

In 2018, Most Rev. Louie P. Galbines, SThD, D.D. was installed as the third Bishop of the Diocese of Kabankalan. He also served as the Chairman of the school's Board of Trustees. In 2019, Rev. Fr. Eugene D. Lucerna II, PhD, RPsy, RGC was appointed as School President. He was the first alumnus (KCC High School Batch 1996) to take over the aforementioned leadership position.

On April 15, 2025, Bishop Galbines appointed Rev. Fr. Richard L. Lachica, MAEd, as the new school president starting Academic Year 2025-2026.
