KCC Student Handbook: Core Values of a KCCian — A, N, S (Part 2 of 2) — Section 1.4
A — ACCOUNTABILITY: A KCCian takes ownership of his/her decisions and actions, contributing responsibly to society and upholding the standards of a globally competent individual.

N — NURTURING: A KCCian cares for God's creation as responsible steward entrusted to protect, sustain, and honor the world He has made.

S — SYNODALITY: A KCCian journeys together with others in communion, fostering collaboration and active participation in the mission of the Church and community.
