3. Emotional Abuse — is sustained, repetitive, and inappropriate emotional responses to a child's need for love, nurturance and care.
4. Sexual Abuse — is the involvement of a child in a sexual activity, either through active involvement or observation. This may include but is not limited to the enticement or threat to a child to engage in an unlawful sexual activity, the exploitation of a child in child prostitution, and the use of a child in pornographic activities, performances and materials.
5. Witnessing family violence — is exposure to actual or threats of verbal and physical violence between family and extended family members.
6. Commercial exploitation — is the use of children in work or other activities for the significant commercial and noncommercial benefit of others. This includes but is not limited to harmful child labor, child prostitution, and the exploitation of children through pornography. (Philippines Republic Act No. 7610, Section 9)
