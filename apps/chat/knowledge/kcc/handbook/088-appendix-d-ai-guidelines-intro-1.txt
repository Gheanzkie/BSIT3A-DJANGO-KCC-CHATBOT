KCC Student Handbook Appendix D: Guidelines on the Use of AI — Introduction (Part 1 of 2)
Over the past decade, educators have increasingly adopted technology to deliver information and instruction online, resulting in varied outcomes. While such tools have expanded educators' reach and provided flexibility in teaching methods, challenges persist related to differences in computer access and proficiency, as well as increased feelings of isolation and the need for self-directed learning. The emergence of Artificial Intelligence (AI)-generated text represents the latest development in higher education, prompting us to reconsider the nature of learning both now and in the future.

AI technology improves access to information and enables the handling of complex tasks, data analysis, and decision-making with minimal human intervention. Therefore, the central question when integrating AI and technology into teaching and learning is: at what point does their use compromise the learning process?
