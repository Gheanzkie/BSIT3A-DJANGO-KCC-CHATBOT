KCC Student Handbook Appendix E: Enhanced No Smoking Policy (OSP-005-2024) — Part 1 of 3
Through this memo, policies governing the use and sale of all tobacco and non-tobacco cigarette products, including electronic nicotine and non-nicotine delivery systems (ENDS/ENNDS), commonly known as e-cigarettes, vapes, vape pens, Julie, hookah smoking, and the like, are in place, and all concerned are enjoined to assist in the strict implementation of these.

The following policies must be strictly implemented and followed:
1. Smoking is absolutely banned within our campus and the Kabankalan Catholic College - Laboratory Complex (KCC-LC), whether inside or outside buildings and parked vehicles.
2. The sale and distribution of all cigarette products and smoking devices is banned within our campus and the KCC-LC.
