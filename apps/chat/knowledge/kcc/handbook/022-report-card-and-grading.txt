KCC Student Handbook: Report Card and Grading System — Sections 2.8-2.9
A report card referring to conduct, diligence, and academic grades will be regularly issued to parents, to keep the student and his/her parents aware of his/her progress or regressions. It should be properly signed by the parent/guardian and will be returned to the adviser not later than two weeks after the release.

Grading System (2.9): The school adopts the grading system in accordance with the current DepEd Order for Kindergarten and Grades 1 to 12.
