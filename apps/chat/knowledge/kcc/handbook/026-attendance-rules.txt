KCC Student Handbook: Attendance Rules — Section 3.1
Regular attendance at all classes is required from all students. When absent, a student has to accomplish all assignments given during his/her absence. A student who has been absent from class for any reason is obliged to bring a letter from his/her parents/guardians stating the reason for the absence incurred. This letter should be countersigned by the adviser before the student may be readmitted to the class.

Students should stay in the school premises during class hours except if he/she has the permission from the principal to leave for a reasonable cause. Students who failed to attend the flag ceremony will not be allowed to attend the first period class (for high school only).
