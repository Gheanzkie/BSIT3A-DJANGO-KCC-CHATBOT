KCC Student Handbook Appendix C: Care, Safety and Protection Protocols — Duty and Responsibility
Excerpt from the Diocesan Education System.

Declaration of Duty: Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. recognizes the worth and dignity of all persons. It recognizes that children are reflective of God's presence and requires vigilance and responsiveness to their needs for care, safety and protection.

Situation of Responsibility: All children enrolled in Kabankalan Catholic College are the direct responsibility of the school while they are in school, during school hours. This responsibility continues when children are on school-sponsored excursions, retreat camps and other similar activities. In such circumstances the school faculty and staff or staff members in charge of activities bear primary responsibility for them.

Care: There is a universal right of all children to have their care needs met. Children have seven needs, which must be met for their healthy development.
