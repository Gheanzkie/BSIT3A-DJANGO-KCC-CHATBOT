KCC Student Handbook: Conduct During School Activities — Section 5.5
Before the activity, students should attend to their personal needs and be properly seated before the start of the program.

During the activity, observe silence and pay attention to the talk, ceremony or performance. Applaud with responsible enthusiasm at the appropriate time. Refrain from loud laughter and boisterous sound.

After the activity, everyone should leave the venue quietly and in an orderly manner.

For safety and security, spearheading organizations must conduct an orientation before the start of the activity.
