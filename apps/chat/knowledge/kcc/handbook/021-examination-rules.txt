KCC Student Handbook: Examination Rules — Section 2.8
Before every examination for every grading period, an examination permit must be secured from the Cashier's office. To take the Periodic examination, a student must present to the proctor the examination permit duly signed by the cashier.

Besides quizzes and tests, a preliminary (prelim) and final examinations are given every grading period. Students should value self-respect and personal integrity; therefore, they are expected to answer the quizzes and examinations honestly. Students are not allowed to leave the examination room during the examination hours except for personal necessities and breaks. They may approach the proctor, not their classmates, if there is a need for clarifications. They should submit all examination papers in accordance with the teacher or proctor's directions.

A student caught cheating will be subjected to disciplinary action and marked zero in the particular test.
