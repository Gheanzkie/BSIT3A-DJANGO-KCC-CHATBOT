KCC Student Handbook Appendix C: Reporting Commitment — Maltreatment Concern (Part 1 of 2)
To enable the greatest degree of school diligence relating to children's care, safety and protection, this Protocols and Procedures document recognizes two levels of awareness: reporting maltreatment concerns and maltreatment allegations.

A maltreatment concern is defined as 'an anxious feeling of worry' that a child is being harmed or is at risk of being harmed through exposure to abuse and/or neglect. This concern may arise from some sensory evidence (sight, hearing, touch, smell) that one or more of a child's care needs are not being appropriately met.
