KCC Student Handbook Appendix D: Why AI Tool Use Is Discouraged at KCC
Adherence to academic honesty is mandatory for all submitted work. Consequently, the use of AI tools is discouraged by KCC due to several reasons:
- Content generated by AI tools may be construed as automated plagiarism, as it draws upon pre-existing texts from undisclosed sources without proper attribution.
- AI models inherently harbor biases, as they are trained on limited datasets, thereby perpetuating errors present in those sources rather than challenging them.
- AI tools possess limitations such as the inability to engage in critical thinking to evaluate criteria and the absence of abductive reasoning to make judgments based on incomplete information.

Any breaches of this policy will be addressed in accordance with KCC's academic integrity guidelines. Penalties for violations may include warning/reprimand, three-day suspension, disciplinary probation and dismissal. It is incumbent upon students to familiarize themselves with the academic integrity policy and ensure that their use of AI-based tools conforms to these regulations.
