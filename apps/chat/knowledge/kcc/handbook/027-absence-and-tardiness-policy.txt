KCC Student Handbook: 80% Attendance Requirement, Absences and Tardiness — Sections 3.1-3.2
In order to receive due credit in any subject, a student must have attended at least 80% of the school days required by the Department of Education. A student who incurred more than 10 successive unexcused absences during the school year will be subject to disciplinary action.

Tardiness (3.2): Habitual tardiness, especially in the first period in the morning and the afternoon, shall not be allowed. Students who are habitually late in coming to class or have incurred five tardiness in a month will be called for a conference together with his/her parent and will be referred to the Discipline Officer. In some cases, the Class Adviser shall call the parent of the student concerned and arrange a home visitation.
