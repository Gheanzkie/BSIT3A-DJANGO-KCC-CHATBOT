KCC Student Handbook Appendix E: Enhanced No Smoking Policy — Part 3 of 3 (Coverage and Penalties)
The absolute ban on smoking covers everyone inside the campus and KCC-LC, including employees (full-time and part-time), students, alumni, visitors, suppliers, contractors, security personnel from agency, and guests of the school, its affiliates, its auxiliary units and its tenants.

Appropriate disciplinary action, in accordance with existing guidelines and due process as stipulated in the Student Daily Guide and the Administrative and Faculty Manual, will be imposed on violators of this policy. All visitors, suppliers, contractors, and couriers inside the campus who violate these policies should be referred to the Discipline Office.
