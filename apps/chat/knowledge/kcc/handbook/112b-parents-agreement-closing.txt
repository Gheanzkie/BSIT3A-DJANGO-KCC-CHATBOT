KCC Student Handbook Appendix J: Parents Agreement with KCC — Closing Undertaking
'We fully accept the right of the school to refuse admission of our son/daughter at any time of the school year, or to refuse promotion or graduation or participation in graduation, if we fail to comply with or abide by any of the above written terms and conditions of this Agreement and those mentioned in our child's Student's Handbook and Agreement, or when our child fails to comply with or abide by any of the terms and conditions of the said Student's Handbook and Agreement, most especially in the exercise of the right and/or prerogative of the school to hold my child/classes under probation, to exclude or expel him, to allow him to graduate without march, to graduate him in summer (which is without march), to fail out, to drop from its rolls, to pass out, or to place my child under summer remedial due to any cause stated therein.'
