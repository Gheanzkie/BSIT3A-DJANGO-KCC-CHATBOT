KCC Student Handbook: Intramural and Extramural Activities — Sections 4.3-4.4
Intramural (4.3): Various intramural competitions are organized within the school. Those students/pupils who are not actively engaged in a particular event are expected to give support to their team. Participants must wear the appropriate intramural uniforms. All students are expected to display a spirit of true sportsmanship in these competitions.

Extramural (4.4): To help develop positive habits and attitudes among students/pupils, the school participates in outside athletic and cultural events. Provided they are maintaining appropriate levels of academic achievement, students/pupils may be selected for membership of such teams.
