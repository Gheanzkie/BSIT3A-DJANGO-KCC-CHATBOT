KCC Student Handbook Appendix G: Waste Disposal Rules — Part 2 of 3
1. Recycling: KCCians must know the importance and benefits of recycling to the environment. Thus, it is mandatory that students, faculty and staff practice recycling, such as the use of scratch papers, plastic, tin and glass containers, and properly dispose of recyclable materials in the recycling bins.
2. Trash Bins: Trash bins are provided on every floor for the proper disposal of non-recyclable waste. It is the responsibility of both students and faculty members to ensure that trash is placed in the correct bins and not left on the classroom floor, armchairs or lobby.
