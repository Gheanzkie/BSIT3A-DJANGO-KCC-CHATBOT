KCC Student Handbook: School Credentials — Section 2.6
A student requesting the following should pass the Cashier's office for clearance accounts: Form 137/SF10 (Transcript of Record); Form 138/SF 9 (Report Card); Certification; Diploma; and other pertinent documents.

Request of Form 137/SF10 implies a transfer of a student's permanent record to another institution. Form 137 will be sent by the school to the school where the student has transferred upon the official request of the said school. Release of requested credentials will be scheduled by the registrar.

Requested credentials will only be released after paying the required fee at the Cashier's office. Certificate of attendance from the class adviser during graduation rehearsals for the new elementary/high school graduate is required before credentials will be released.
