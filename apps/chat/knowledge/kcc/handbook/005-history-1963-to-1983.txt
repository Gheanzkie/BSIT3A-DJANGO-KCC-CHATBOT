KCC Student Handbook: History of KCC — St. Paul Sisters Era (1963 to 1983) — Section 1.2
Through the untiring efforts of Fr. Cronin, a general college and an elementary education was opened in the school year 1963-1964. Two years after, he was able to get the services of the St. Paul Sisters of Chartres, to assist in the management of the school. Their experience in school management was an asset. Through them, Kabankalan College became the center of learning in the southern part of Negros Occidental.

The College department was built on a hectare of land donated by the Zayco family. In 1983 the management services of the Sisters of St. Paul terminated and the school administration shifted to the hands of the Diocesan priests headed by Msgr. Josefino I. Iledan as President and assisted by the Presentation Sisters.
