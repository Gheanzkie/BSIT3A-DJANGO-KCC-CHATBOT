KCC Student Handbook: School Philosophy (Part 1 of 2) — Section 1.1
Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. is committed to forming socially conscious, responsible, and service-oriented individuals, guided by the teachings of Christ. They embody a profound understanding of the challenges and opportunities inherent in a rapidly evolving world.

As an evangelizing arm of the Diocese of Kabankalan, KCC seeks to proclaim and spread the teachings of the Church. The institution shepherds students and the community toward a deeper relationship with Christ; strengthening faith that transforms lives through service, compassion, and steadfast devotion to Christian values.

As a preferential option for the poor, Kabankalan Catholic College affirms that education is a universal right. Rooted in a curriculum that integrates the arts, sciences, and the Gospel, a KCC education fosters well-rounded development — one that nurtures each person's mind, body, and soul within a safe and supportive environment.
