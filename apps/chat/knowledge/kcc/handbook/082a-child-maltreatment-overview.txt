KCC Student Handbook Appendix C: Child Maltreatment — Overview
Child maltreatment is deemed to have occurred when there is a violation of one or more of the seven care needs. Maltreatment is a term used to describe acts which harm children. There are two types of child maltreatment: Child Abuse and Child Neglect.

Child Abuse consists of actions towards a child which result in harm towards a child. There are six types of child abuse. Children are often exposed to multiple forms of abuse. Abuse can occur through the actual occurrence of the abuse or through the threat of abuse. The types of abuse are listed in the 'Types of Child Abuse' files.
