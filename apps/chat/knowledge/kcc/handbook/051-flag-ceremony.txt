KCC Student Handbook: Flag Ceremony — Section 5.1
The school day begins with the morning assembly and flag ceremony at 6:50 a.m. All students must attend this ceremony punctually. At the first bell, all students move to the quadrangle and form their lines. The Class Beadle/Adviser checks the attendance.

Every student should show the value of nationalism by singing the National Anthem and reciting the Pledge of Allegiance properly. At the end of the flag ceremony, students should go to their respective rooms quietly.
