KCC Student Handbook Appendix C: Reporting Commitment — Recognitions and Allegation (Part 2 of 2)
In translating a concern into a reporting action, the school recognizes:
- It is not the responsibility of the reporter to determine whether there are sufficient grounds to determine whether there exists a child maltreatment allegation.
- It is through the responsibility to report and the responsibility of school management to undertake an assessment, that a determination will be made as to whether or not the events constitute a child maltreatment allegation.
- It is the responsibility of management to determine and implement an appropriate child safety response if required.

A maltreatment allegation is a specific statement that child abuse or neglect has occurred.
