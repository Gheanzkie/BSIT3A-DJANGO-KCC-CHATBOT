KCC Student Handbook: Door Pass and Vehicle Pass — Sections 5.6-5.7
Door Pass (5.6): Students must stay inside their rooms during class hours. However, for personal necessity or any valid reason, only one to two students are permitted to go out of the classroom using a door pass issued by the high school principal.

Vehicle Pass (5.7): A vehicle pass is issued to all students/pupils who come to school in their own transport. Vehicle owners must secure a vehicle pass, otherwise they are not allowed to enter the school campus.
