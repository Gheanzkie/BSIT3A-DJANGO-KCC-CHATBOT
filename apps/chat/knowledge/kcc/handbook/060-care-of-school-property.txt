KCC Student Handbook: Care of School Property, Classroom and Facilities — Section 5.11
Interest and pride in the school should prompt KCC students/pupils to regard the school property with care and to keep the buildings and the campus neat and clean. Any damage done by the student/pupil to the school property or of others shall be repaired at the student's expense. The classroom is a place for study and its facilities should be used properly. Outside class hours, students/pupils who wish to engage in serious study and quiet preparation for class should use the library. If they have to leave their bags or other items inside the room, students/pupils must secure their valuables. Bags, books or other items are not allowed on the benches outside the rooms and in the corridors.
