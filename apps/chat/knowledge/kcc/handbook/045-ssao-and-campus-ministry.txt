KCC Student Handbook: SSAO/PAO and Campus Ministry Organizations — Sections 4.1-4.2
The Supreme Student Affairs Organizations (SSAO)/Pupil Affairs Organization (PAO) is one of the two highest student/pupil organizations in the campus. Its role is to give support to the school in all scholastics, sports, spiritual and other co- and extracurricular activities.

The Campus Ministry Organization is the other student/pupil organization of highest authority in the school. Its role is to support the school in all its religious activities.
