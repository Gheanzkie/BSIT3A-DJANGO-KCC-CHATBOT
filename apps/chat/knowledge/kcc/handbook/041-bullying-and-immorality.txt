KCC Student Handbook: Bullying and Immorality — Sections 3.12-3.13
Bullying (3.12): Any student caught bullying will be dealt with accordingly based on the school's anti-bullying policy.

Immorality (3.13): To highlight the importance of human integrity, every student is expected to observe the Catholic moral standards. Any student found engaging in any immoral actions or gestures will be subjected to disciplinary action.
