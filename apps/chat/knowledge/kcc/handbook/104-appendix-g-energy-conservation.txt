KCC Student Handbook Appendix G: Conservation of Energy and Campus Cleanliness — Part 1 of 3
Energy Conservation and Campus Cleanliness:
1. Class Advisers: Classrooms are the primary responsibility of class advisers. Class advisers must instruct students to conserve energy and maintain cleanliness and order in the classrooms. Added to this, class advisers must monitor the cleanliness and orderliness of classrooms on a daily basis, and see to it that electric fans and LED TVs are turned off after use.
2. Students: Students must actively participate in energy conservation and in keeping their classroom clean and orderly. They should turn off fans and LED TVs after using them, dispose of trash properly, arrange armchairs, refrain from vandalizing armchairs and walls, and refrain from using any types of tapes on walls or furniture.
