KCC Student Handbook: The School Seal — Symbols Explained (Part 1 of 2) — Section 1.3
The twelve (12) waves at the rim of the logo represent the twelve (12) apostles tasked by Christ to teach and evangelize.

The school motto 'TEACH YE ALL NATIONS' expresses KCC's untiring response to Christ's call for evangelization. Every KCCian should then strive to be a model Christian and true patriot. In this way, they will be honest and responsible citizens.

The two stars in the seal symbolize the guiding light of Catholic Education. One star represents KCC's identity, being rooted in Catholic faith and values. The other star represents its mission, to spread knowledge, truth, and service to others.
