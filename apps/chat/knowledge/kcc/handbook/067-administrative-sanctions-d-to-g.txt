KCC Student Handbook: Administrative Sanctions — Exclusion, Restitution, Retribution, Probation (7.3 D-G)
D. EXCLUSION / DISMISSAL — is a penalty in which the school is allowed to exclude or drop the name of the erring student from the school roll for being undesirable, and transfer credentials are immediately issued.

E. RESTITUTION — is a reimbursement or payment for damage, destruction or misappropriation of the school property or on any members of the school community. This supplements principal sanctions.

F. RETRIBUTION — requires recompense for misbehavior. The recompense may be in the form of service or community work related to the offense committed. This sanction is imposed alone or coexistent with other sanctions.

G. DISCIPLINARY PROBATION — is the period in which the erring student is given a last chance after promising good behavior, and is required to fulfill specified conditions as agreed upon by him/her and his/her parents with the school through the Office of the Discipline/Values Formation Committee.
