KCC Student Handbook: Disciplinary Committee and Categories of Sanctions — Sections 7.2, 7.4, 7.5
Disciplinary Committee (7.2): The disciplinary committee is a body constituted by the Principal, the Discipline Officer, Advisers and other selected school officials.

Disciplinary Actions / Sanction Selection (7.4): The Committee on Discipline shall have the right to determine the kind of action appropriate to the case, taking into account the following factors: a. previous record of the student; b. gravity of the offense; c. established precedents.

Categories of Sanctions (7.5): A. Warning / Reprimand; B. Post / Community Service; C. 1-day Suspension; D. 2-Day Suspension; E. 3-Day Suspension; F. 5-Day Suspension with Disciplinary Probation; G. Dismissal.
