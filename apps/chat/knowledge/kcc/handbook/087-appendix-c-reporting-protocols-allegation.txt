KCC Student Handbook Appendix C: Reporting Protocols — Maltreatment Allegation (Faculty and Staff)
Responsibility of Faculty and Staff in reporting a maltreatment allegation. The person considering making an allegation should identify in his/her mind:
- What type of maltreatment is believed may have occurred, is occurring or is at risk of occurring.
- Abuse — Physical; Psychological; Emotional; Sexual; Witnessing family violence; Commercial exploitation.
- Neglect — Physical; Psychological; Emotional; Cognitive; Moral.

He/she should write down notes to remember what was heard, touched or smelt (include time, date, names, location), and report to the School Principal/Director.
