KCC Student Handbook: Drop-out or Withdrawal of Enrollment — Section 2.5
A. Entrance fee is fully payable by the enrollee.

B. Payments in case of withdrawal from enrollment after the opening of classes are as follows:
1. 20% of the total school fees — if the student withdraws within two weeks after the opening of classes, whether he/she attended the classes or not.
2. 50% of the school fees — if the student withdraws after two weeks after the opening of classes, whether he/she attended the classes or not.
3. 100% of the school fees — if the student withdraws after four weeks after the opening of classes, whether he/she attended the classes or not.
