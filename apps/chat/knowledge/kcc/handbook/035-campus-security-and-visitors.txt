KCC Student Handbook: Campus Security and Visitors — Section 3.6
Only bona fide students of the school shall be allowed inside the school campus. No visitors shall be allowed inside the campus during school hours except for valid reasons. Visitors who have valid reasons to be in the campus shall be required to sign in the log book of the security service. Furthermore, visitors must wear the issued 'Visitor's Pass'. Teachers shall confer with parents/guardians or entertain visitors during their off period. No students or visitors shall be allowed inside the school building and the premises after the last class period has ended.
