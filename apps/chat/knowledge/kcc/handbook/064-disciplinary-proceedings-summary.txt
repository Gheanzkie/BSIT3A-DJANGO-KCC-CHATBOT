KCC Student Handbook: Disciplinary Proceedings — Summary Proceedings — Section 7.1
Subject to compliance with the requirements of due process and school regulation, the procedure for disciplinary action against a student shall be summarized in nature. The student shall be assisted in the proceedings by his/her parent/guardian or counsel.

Parents concerned shall be informed properly and shall be called to attend the conference of their child's case in the Office of the Principal.

Erring students will be reprimanded or given a warning for first and second offenses but may be given higher administrative penalties if they become habitually violators of the school rules and regulations.

Restitution and retribution may be required from the guilty respondent in addition to other disciplinary sanctions.

The Office of the Principal, through the Committee on Discipline, may place an erring student on disciplinary/academic probation whenever necessary.
