KCC Student Handbook: Dress Code for Civilian Attire — Not Acceptable Clothing — Section 3.6
The following are NOT ACCEPTABLE:
- Tops which are see-through, sleeveless, strapless, backless, and with plunging necklines
- Jeans with holes, shred or rips above knee level
- Low rise or soggy pants, revealing undergarments
- Miniskirts, shorts above knee-level, leggings, pajamas
- Slippers

Clothing must not display alcohol, violence, discrimination, drug or sexual references. Cross-dressing is not allowed.
