KCC Student Handbook: Student Activities — General Rules — Section 4
The school shall be responsible for the maintenance of school discipline among the students inside the school campus as well as outside the school premises whenever they are engaged in authorized school activities. Classrooms are to be used for academic purposes only. No group actions like boycott of classes/demonstrations against any issue, person or teacher will be allowed.

Co-curricular activity shall be subjected to prior approval from the school head. Meetings/assemblies, convocation and activities shall be held in the presence of and with the knowledge of the principal, guidance counselor, teachers, or club advisers/coaches.
