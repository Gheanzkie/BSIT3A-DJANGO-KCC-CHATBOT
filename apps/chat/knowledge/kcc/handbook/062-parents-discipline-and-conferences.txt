KCC Student Handbook: Parents' Conduct — Discipline Conferences and Appointments — Section 6
Minor infringements of school rules and discipline are usually dealt with by the teachers and/or other staff members. More serious breaches of discipline need to be dealt with by the Disciplinary Committee. Parents will be invited to attend the hearings of the committee together with their son/daughter and to speak with the appropriate school officials, e.g. the Classroom Adviser, the Discipline Officer, the Principal, about any misconduct of their student/pupil and the appropriate penalty that the school Principal imposes. This may include reparation for any damage done.

Parents who come to school to set an appointment with a teacher or a student can only do so through the Principal's Office. A parent who comes to school to pay fees should directly go to the Cashier's Office. Parents who come to school should observe the school's dress code.
