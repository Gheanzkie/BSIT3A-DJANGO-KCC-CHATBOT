KCC Hymn — Lyrics
Composer: Sr. Marie Josephine Antonio, SPC. Lyricist: Miss Ester Besas.

Kabankalan Catholic College
Our haven of pain, of joy and glory
To your teachings and ideals
We'll ever cling
Amidst the oils and trials life may bring
Alma Mater Dear all hail to you!
All hail to you.
Guardian and light of our young lives
Struggles and pains and frustrations
Are to us the pillars to lean on.
For in your portals and inspirations
We've built our hopes, our dreams and our ambitions,
Even when we'll leave you dear school
Beautiful memories will linger on.
