KCC Student Handbook: Leave of Absence — Section 3.4
With the permission of the Principal, students/pupils may be granted a leave of absence for valid reasons such as study, illness, family relocation, and other justifiable circumstances. Such requests must be supported by appropriate documentation and a written agreement duly signed by the parent/guardian and the school authorities, stating the terms and conditions of the leave. Upon approval, the Principal shall issue a leave pass for the approved leave of absence.
