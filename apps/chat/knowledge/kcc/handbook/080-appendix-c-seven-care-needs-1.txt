KCC Student Handbook Appendix C: The Seven Care Needs of Children (Part 1 of 2)
1. Physical Care — food, shelter, clothing, medical care and freedom from physical harm.
2. Psychological Care — the need for a child to develop: self-identity (as a unique person within cultural and community context); self-worth (positive value of self); self-capacity (an individual belief that the child can positively undertake education, work and social tasks and actions in a socially acceptable and appropriate manner).
3. Emotional Care — the need for children to receive nurturance, love and affection to enable them to develop appropriate emotions toward themselves and towards others in society.
4. Cognitive Care — the need for children to receive cognitive and intellectual stimulation through formal and informal education, to enable him/her to develop his/her learning and thinking skills and contribute these skills and abilities towards the development of society.
