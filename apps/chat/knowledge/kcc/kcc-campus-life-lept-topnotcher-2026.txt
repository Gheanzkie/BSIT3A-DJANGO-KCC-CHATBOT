KCC Alumna Ranks 8th in March 2026 LEPT (Board Exam Topnotcher)
Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. celebrates alumna Kristel Clair T. Paltu-ob, LPT, who secured a spot among the top examinees in the March 2026 Licensure Examination for Professional Teachers (LEPT) - ranked eighth nationwide with a 93.20% rating. Her achievement reflects the institution's pursuit of academic excellence. KCC commends her perseverance, describing the feat as both a personal success and a significant achievement for the school. Her success inspires aspiring teachers, showing that determination and discipline can lead to remarkable achievements on the national stage.
