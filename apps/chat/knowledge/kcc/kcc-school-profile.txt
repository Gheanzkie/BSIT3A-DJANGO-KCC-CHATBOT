KCC School Profile - Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc.
Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. (KCC) is a Christ-centered Catholic educational institution in Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental, Philippines.
- Founded: 1927, as "Kabankalan Institute" (later renamed Kabankalan Private Academy; formerly Kabankalan Academy).
- Motto: "Teach Ye All Nations" (Matthew 28:19).
- Tagline: "Catholic Education: Our Identity, Our Mission".
- Run by the Diocese of Kabankalan.
- Offers: Basic Education (Elementary K-6 and Junior High School 7-10), Senior High School (Grades 11-12, Academic and TVL tracks), and College programs.
- 32 program offerings in total, including 13 college degree programs.
- Close to a century of Catholic education, known for academic excellence, faith formation, community service, and holistic development.
