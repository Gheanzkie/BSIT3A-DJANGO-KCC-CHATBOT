KCC Admissions and Enrollment Process
Mga hakbang sa enrollment (4 steps) at kumpletong requirements:
1. Submit Requirements - Prepare and submit your application form along with the required documents.
2. Entrance Examination - Take the entrance examination to assess your academic readiness.
3. Interview - Attend a brief interview with the admissions committee.
4. Enrollment - Complete your enrollment and officially become a KCC student.
Requirements (bring on enrollment): Completed Application Form; Report Card / Form 138; Certificate of Good Moral Character; PSA Birth Certificate; 2x2 ID Photos (4 pieces); Honorable Dismissal (for transferees).
Enrollment is open - secure your slot for the upcoming academic year. Visit the Registrar's Office or contact the admissions team for details. Scholarship programs and financial assistance are available for qualifying students.
