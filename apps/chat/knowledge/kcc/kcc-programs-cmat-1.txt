KCC College Programs - Management, Accountancy and Technology (Part 1)
Ang mga sumusunod ay college courses/kurso (degree programs) ng KCC sa ilalim ng College of Management, Accountancy and Technology (CMAT) - Part 1.
BS in Business Administration (BSBA) - Majors: Financial Management, Marketing Management. Careers: Financial Analyst, Marketing Manager, HR Specialist, Entrepreneur, Business Consultant. Prepares students for careers in business and management: financial analysis, marketing strategy, organizational management, entrepreneurship.
BS in Information Technology (BSIT) - Careers: Software Developer, Network Administrator, Database Administrator, IT Consultant, Web Developer. Develops future-ready IT professionals with skills in software development, networking, database management, and emerging technologies, with hands-on experience building real-world applications.
BS in Accountancy (BSA) - Careers: Certified Public Accountant, External Auditor, Tax Consultant, Financial Controller, Government Auditor. Develops competent and ethical accountants prepared for the CPA licensure examination, with expertise in financial accounting, auditing, taxation, and management advisory services.
