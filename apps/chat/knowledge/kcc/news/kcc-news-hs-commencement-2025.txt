# KCC High School Department Holds 78th Commencement & Moving Up Ceremonies
Date: March 2025 | Category: Graduation
The Kabankalan Catholic College High School Department held its 78th Commencement Exercises and 5th Moving Up Ceremonies, celebrating the hard work and dedication of its graduating students.
The ceremonies honored both the Junior High School completers (Moving Up) and the Senior High School graduates (Commencement Exercises). Students, parents, faculty, and guests gathered to witness this milestone.
Graduates were reminded of KCC's core values - Kindness, Christ-Centeredness, Creativity, Integrity, Accountability, Nurturing, and Synodality - as they embark on their next chapter in life.
Congratulations to the KCC Class of 2025!
