# KCC FAQ - Teachers, Guro, Faculty, Tuition Fees, Scholarships, Offices
Teachers, guro, at faculty: Hindi nakalista sa website ang listahan ng teachers/faculty ng KCC; para sa faculty concerns, kontakin ang Registrar's Office o school administration. Para sa opisina ng dean, tingnan ang dean office notes.
Tuition at fees (bayad, bayarin, magkano ang tuition): Hindi naka-publish ang tuition fee schedule sa KCC website. Para sa aktwal na tuition at fees, kontakin ang Registrar's Office o Accounting Office: (032) 4712-479, kcc_1927@yahoo.com.
Scholarships: May scholarship programs at financial assistance para sa qualifying students (ayon sa enrollment announcement) - magtanong sa Registrar's Office.
Iba pang madalas itanong: courses/kurso/programs - tingnan ang programs master list; enrollment at requirements - tingnan ang admissions; registrar, admissions team, counseling office - tingnan ang offices quick guide; lokasyon at contact - tingnan ang contacts.
