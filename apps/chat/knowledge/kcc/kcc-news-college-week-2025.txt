College Department Holds Intramurals and College Week
Date: January 2025 | Category: Event
The KCC College Department held its annual College Week, bringing together students from all programs for a week of friendly competition, academic contests, and cultural showcases.
Events included basketball, volleyball, and table tennis tournaments, as well as quiz bees, debate competitions, essay writing contests, and talent shows. Each program fielded teams and representatives, fostering camaraderie and school spirit.
The College Week culminated in an awarding ceremony recognizing outstanding performances in both athletic and academic competitions.
