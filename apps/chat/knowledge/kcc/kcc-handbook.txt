1.1 PHILOSOPHY, VISION and MISSION
School Philosophy
Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. is committed to forming socially conscious,
responsible, and service-oriented individuals, guided by the teachings of Christ.
They embody a profound understanding of the challenges and opportunities
inherent in a rapidly evolving world.
As an evangelizing arm of the Diocese of Kabankalan, KCC seeks to proclaim
and spread the teachings of the Church. The institution shepherds students and
the community toward a deeper relationship with Christ; strengthening faith that
transforms lives through service, compassion, and steadfast devotion to Christian
values.
As a preferential option for the poor, Kabankalan Catholic College affirms that
education is a universal right. Rooted in a curriculum that integrates the arts,
sciences, and the Gospel, a KCC education fosters well-rounded development—
one that nurtures each person’s mind, body, and soul within a safe and supportive
environment.
As an educational institution, KCC seeks academic excellence with a strong
moral foundation characterized by integrity, compassion, and generosity. Our
students and graduates are equipped with the skills, knowledge, and disposition
necessary to contribute positively to society and to respond effectively to the needs
of the Diocese, the nation, and the global community.
KCC aims to achieve all these through the delivery of exceptional instruction;
the development of suitable learning spaces and facilities; the conduct of
innovative research and creative projects; and the engagement in strong and
purposeful outreach.
Vision
Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc., as builder of the future,
is a Christ-centered learning community of accountable and socially responsible
individuals, that leads positive change, responds to and serves the mission
of the Diocese, the nation, and the world.
Mission
KCC commits to provide quality and inclusive Catholic education,
rooted in the Gospel and driven by innovative research and meaningful
community engagements, empowering its members to become
effective agents of societal transformation.
1.2 A BRIEF HISTORY OF Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc.
In 1927 a group of incorporators gathered to establish a secondary school
named Kabankalan Institute which was later changed to Kabankalan Private
Academy.
The first commencement exercises of the school was in 1940. Classes
were suspended during the Second World War (1941-1944) and were resumed in
the school year 1945-1946. The Board of Trustees got a permit to open a Junior
Normal College which lasted only for three years due to poor enrollment.
In 1953, the Columban Fathers through Fr. Thomas J. Cronin, parish priest
of Kabankalan, thought of putting up a new school but later negotiated to buy
2
Kabankalan Academy. However, most of the stockholders donated their shares
in exchange for scholarship privileges.
When the Columban Fathers took over, the first decision they made was to
transfer the site of the school to the space near the church. The school directorship
under the Columban Fathers was not a permanent position. The change of the
directorship went with the reshuffling of the parish priest. This was the standing
regulation among the Columban Fathers.
Through the untiring efforts of Fr. Cronin, a general college and an
elementary education was opened in the school year 1963-1964. Two years after,
he was able to get the services of the St. Paul Sisters of Chartres, to assist in the
management of the school. Their experience in school management was an asset.
Through them, Kabankalan College became the center of learning in the southern
part of Negros Occidental
The College department was built on a hectare of land donated by the
Zayco family. In 1983 the management services of the Sisters of St. Paul
terminated and the school administration shifted to the hands of the Diocesan
priests headed by Msgr. Josefino I. Iledan as President and assisted by the
Presentation Sisters.
On March 14, 1987, while the town was celebrating its 80th Foundation Day,
a fire of dubious origin razed the high school building, the parish convent and the
Old Catholic church to the ground. The catastrophe brought untold financial
losses to the school. Massive rehabilitation and reconstruction were immediately
instituted as the school faced the opening of the next school year. Meanwhile the
high school department was temporarily relocated to the College campus while the
permanent three-storey building on the newly acquired lot adjacent to the College
was under construction.
An additional phase to the physical structure of the college is the St.
Columban Arts Center which serves as the socio-cultural venue not only for the
students but also for the community. Behind is the annex, a concrete building that
houses the new college canteen and the textbook center on the ground floor and
the College library on the upper floor.
In 1988, Kabankalan was made a new Diocese with Most Reverend Vicente
M. Navarra, D.D. as its First Bishop. That year, the College obtained the services
of the Religious of the Good Shepherd who served for a year. For 8 years, the
senior academic positions were taken by the lay members of the academic
community.
In 1996, the Carmelite Sisters of Our Lady (Carm, O.L.) were invited by Most
Rev. Vicente Navarra, D.D., Bishop of Kabankalan at the same time Chairman of
the Board of Trustees, to assist Msgr. Rogelio B. Cruz, the School President, in the
management of the school.
Most Reverend Patricio A. Buzon, SDB, D.D., was installed as the second
Bishop of the Diocese of Kabankalan in 2003. In 2005, he administered the
School as the Chairman of the Board of Trustees and as School President. Under
his management, the KCC-Technological Center situated in Barangay Talubangi,
Kabankalan City was established in 2009. In 2011, Rev. Fr. Radny T. Bayog, PhD,
was appointed as School President of KCC.
In 2018, Most Rev. Louie P. Galbines, SThD, D.D. was installed as the third
Bishop of the Diocese of Kabankalan. He also served as the Chairman of the
school's Board of Trustees. In 2019, Rev. Fr. Eugene D. Lucerna II, PhD, RPsy,
RGC was appointed as School President. He was the first alumnus (KCC High
School Batch 1996) to take over the aforementioned leadership position.
On April 15, 2025, Bishop Galbines appointed Rev. Fr. Richard L. Lachica,
MAEd, as the new school president starting Academic Year 2025-2026.

1. 3. THE SCHOOL SEAL
The twelve (12) waves at the rim of the logo represent the twelve (12)
apostles tasked by Christ to teach and evangelize.
The school motto “TEACH YE ALL NATIONS” expresses KCC’s untiring
response to Christ’s call for evangelization. Every KCCian should then strive to be
a model Christian and true patriot. In this way, they will be honest and responsible
citizens.
The two stars in the seal symbolize the guiding light of Catholic Education.
One star represents KCC’s identity, being rooted in Catholic faith and values. The
other star represents its mission, to spread knowledge, truth, and service to others.
The Philippine map, specifically the island of Negros, represents the
immediate world KCC aims to evangelize. The other islands represent the world
which KCC hopes to reach out to.
The dark blue and gold seal symbolizes wisdom, integrity, and excellence.
Dark blue represents knowledge and moral strength, while gold embodies the
eternal light of Christ, guiding the KCCians to success and dignity. This version
highlights KCC’s prestige and commitment to form graduates who embody truth
and honor.

2 GENERAL INFORMATION
Enrollment at Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. is a privilege. Therefore, the
School Administration reserves the right to admit students/pupils on the basis of
moral character, academic standing and other credentials.
Any student/pupil admitted to the school is subject to the regulations of the
school as set out in this Daily Guide and in other appropriate government and
school legislation. Although Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. is a Catholic school,
it is happy to enroll students of other religious beliefs, provided they will follow its
rules and regulations.
The enrollment of students/pupils for High School/ Elementary will normally
be conducted during the registration days as indicated in the school calendar and
in the school information boards. Enrollment at other times can be arranged
through the School Registrar/Principal. Students/Pupils must make a personal
appearance for the enrollment procedures.
2. 1 ADMISSION POLICIES
Admission to the Kinder, Grade 7 and Grade 11 is open to all on a first come,
first served basis. Test and oral interview will be given before the enrollment.
Admission of transferees with no failing grades in any subject from Grades 1 to 12
will also be on a first come first served basis, provided there are vacant slots.
An old student with two or more failures in the final grades will not be
accepted unless he or she presents a certificate of attendance in a summer class
with passing grades for Grade 8 to 12.
A student under probation who has not shown improvement will not be
admitted.
2.2 ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS
A. Students /Pupils seeking admission to kindergarten, grades 7 and 11 should
present the following:
Kindergarten Certificate for Grade 1
Elementary Report Card for Grade 7
Certificate of Good Moral Character for Grade 7
PSA Birth Certificate (photocopy)
Baptismal/Dedication Certificate
2x2 picture (2 copies)
Duly accomplished application form for administration
B. Old students should present a report card and those with failures in the final
grades should present a certificate of attendance in a summer class with passing
grades.
C. Transferees should present the following:
Report Card
Certificate of eligibility to transfer
Certificate of good moral character
PSA Birth Certificate (photocopy)
Baptismal/Dedication Certificate
Certificate of ESC-PEAC from a previous private school for Gr 8 to 12.

2.8 EXAMINATION / REPORT CARD
Before every examination for every grading period, an examination permit
must be secured from the Cashier’s office. To take the Periodic examination, a
student must present to the proctor the examination permit duly signed by the
cashier.
Besides quizzes and tests, a preliminary (prelim) and final examinations are
given every grading period. Students should value self-respect and personal
integrity. Therefore, they are expected to answer the quizzes and examinations
honestly. Students are not allowed to leave the examination room during the
examination hours except for personal necessities and breaks. They may
approach the proctor, not their classmates, if there is a need for clarifications. They
should submit all examination papers in accordance with the teacher or proctor’s
directions.
A student caught cheating will be subjected to disciplinary action and marked
zero in the particular test. To keep the student and his/her parents aware of his/her
progress or regressions, a report card referring to conduct, diligence, and
academic grades, will be regularly issued to parents. It should be properly signed
by the parent/guardian and will be returned to the adviser not later than two weeks
after the release.
2.9 GRADING SYSTEM
The school adopts the grading system in accordance with the current DepEd
Order for Kindergarten and Grades 1 to 12.
2.10 HONORS AND AWARDS
Selection of honor students in any grade level will be based on DepEd Order
No.36, s. 2016 (unless revoked by a new issuance of DepEd order). The award for
Academic Excellence is given to learners from Kindergarten to Grade 12 who have
attained an average of at least 90 and with a cut-off grade of 85 in all learning
areas in all quarters and will be given due recognition by the school at the end of
every school year.
Students who excelled in co-curricular and extra-curricular activities will be
given due award/recognition at the end of every school year as determined by the
school committee.
A loyalty award will be given to any graduating student who has a continuous
tenure from nursery to Grade 12.
2.11 GRADUATION
A candidate for graduation should attend the graduation rehearsals
punctually and regularly. A certificate of attendance will be issued to students by
their respective advisers as a requisite for student's claim of his/her credentials
from the registrar. The school prescribes the Graduation attire.
2.12 NOTICES
School notices are posted on various bulletin boards and the school's official
social media accounts. It is the student’s/pupil’s responsibility to read these notices.
Bulletin boards and school’s official social media accounts are for official use only.
Tampering with any of them is a serious offense.
Only with the Principal’s permission may student/pupil post notices or posters
on the school’s notice boards.

3 SCHOOL DISCIPLINE
3.1 ATTENDANCE
Regular attendance at all classes is required from all students. When absent,
student has to accomplish all assignments given during his/her absence. A student
who has been absent from class for any reason is obliged to bring a letter from
his/her parents/guardians stating the reason for the absence incurred. This letter
should be countersigned by the adviser before the student may be readmitted to
the class.
Students should stay in the school premises during class hours except if
he/she has the permission from the principal to leave for a reasonable cause.
Students who failed to attend the flag ceremony will not be allowed to attend
the first period class(for high school only). In order to receive due credit in any
subject, a student must have attended at least 80% of the school days required by
the Department of Education.
A student who incurred more than 10 successive unexcused absences during
the school year will be subject to disciplinary action.
3.2 TARDINESS
Habitual tardiness especially in the first period in the morning and the
afternoon shall not be allowed. Students who are habitually late in coming to class
or have incurred five tardiness in a month will be called for a conference together
with his/her parent and will be referred to the Discipline Officer. In some cases, the
Class Adviser shall call the parent of the student concerned and arrange a home
visitation.
3.3 DELAYED TEACHER
If a teacher is not in class after the second bell rings, the Class Beadle or
other class officer must inform the Principal’s office of the teacher’s absence. The
Class Mayor and other officers shall be responsible for the good order of the class
until the teacher arrives or some alternative arrangement will be made.
3.4 LEAVE OF ABSENCE
With the permission of the Principal, students/pupils may be granted a leave
of absence for valid reasons such as study, illness, family relocation, and other
justifiable circumstances. Such requests must be supported by appropriate
documentation and a written agreement duly signed by the parent/guardian and
the school authorities, stating the terms and conditions of the leave. Upon approval,
the Principal shall issue a leave pass for the approved leave of absence.
3.5 SCHOOL UNIFORM, DRESS CODE AND GROOMING
The prescribed school uniform must be worn from Mondays to Fridays.
Students must observe the following:
HAIR CUT POLICY
a. Neat and well-groomed hair. Allowed haircut is only barber’s cut with
no lines or fashionable haircut designs.
b. No wearing of multiple earrings
c. No wearing of earrings for male students
d. No hair dye exceeding color spectrum 6
e. No cross-dressing
f. Having tattoos is strongly discouraged.
Students are issued IDs at the beginning of the school year. No ID, No Entry
policy is observed in the campus. The ID card must be worn prior to the student’s
entrance to the campus. It may be clipped, pinned or worn with string and visibly
9
displayed on the front of the body with the student's picture facing front. It must
also be worn in other school related activities outside the school.
The ID is strictly non-transferable. The ID must not be altered or mutilated. ID
not worn in the school campus will be confiscated by any teacher or staff and must
be turned over to the Discipline Officer. All confiscated IDs must be claimed at the
Discipline Office not later than 48 hours after confiscation.
Lost ID should be replaced immediately. It should be reported to the adviser
for issuance of temporary pass and the following requirements must be complied
and submitted to the VPSM:
Affidavit of loss
Conference with the adviser
Replacement fee

During school activities, students may be allowed to wear civilian attire
provided that it conforms to the following:
The following are acceptable:
l Blouses with sleeves, t-shirts, polo shirts, tops covering the midriffs when
standing naturally
l Strapless and sleeveless tops may be worn with a cardigan/sweater
that full covers all areas
l Skirts, dresses and shorts must be knee-length or lower
l Mid-thigh or lower athletic shorts (allowed only during sports
competitions)
l Jeans, pants, jeans with holes, shred or rips at knee level or lower
l Sandals, step-in, shoes
The following are not acceptable:
l Tops which are see-through, sleeveless, strapless, backless, and with
plunging necklines
l Jeans with holes, shred or rips above knee level
l Low rise or soggy pants, revealing undergarments
l Miniskirts, shorts above knee-level, leggings, pajamas
l Slippers
Clothing must not display alcohol, violence, discrimination, drug or sexual
references. Crossdressing is not allowed.
3.6 CAMPUS SECURITY
Only bona fide students of the school shall be allowed inside the school
campus. No visitors shall be allowed inside the campus during school hours except
for valid reasons. Visitors who have valid reasons to be in the campus shall be
required to sign in the log book of the security service. Furthermore, visitors must
wear the issued “Visitor’s Pass”. Teachers shall confer with parents/guardians or
entertain visitors during their off period. No students or visitors shall be allowed
inside the school building and the premises after the last class period has ended.
3.7 NO SMOKING POLICY
The school is a smoke-free campus. No student is allowed to bring in
cigarettes or e-cigarettes and smoke in the school premises. Students caught
violating this policy is subject to disciplinary action.
3.8 ANTI-ALCOHOL INTOXICATION POLICY
10
No students are allowed to enter the school premises under the influence of
alcohol. Possession of any intoxicating drink is also prohibited. Offenders will be
dealt with accordingly.
3.9 POSTING ON SOCIAL MEDIA
Uploading of indecent and vulgar pictures, videos and statements or
comments is strongly prohibited. Violators will be subjected to disciplinary action.
3.10 AFFILIATION TO UNAUTHORIZED ORGANIZATIONS
No student is allowed to join organizations, associations and groups which
are not recognized by the school. Infraction of this policy is subject to disciplinary
action.
3.11 PARTICIPATION TO BEAUTY PAGEANTS AND OTHER SIMILAR
ACTIVITIES
To safeguard the dignity of human person, bona fide students are not allowed
to join in any beauty pageants, contests, search and other similar activities outside
of the school without the school’s approval. Violators will be dealt with accordingly.
3.12 BULLYING
Any student caught bullying will be dealt accordingly based on the school’s
anti-bullying policy.
3.13 IMMORALITY
To highlight the importance of human integrity, every student is expected to
observe the Catholic moral standards. Any student found engaging in any immoral
actions or gestures will be subjected to disciplinary action.
3.14 USE OF GADGETS
Students are discouraged to use any gadgets during class hours unless
required by the teacher for instructional purposes. Non – observance of this policy
is subject to disciplinary actions. Confiscated gadgets will be turned over for
safekeeping to the Discipline Officer or Principal for five (5) working days.
3.15 INSPECTION
For safety and security, security guards and school watch personnel shall

4 STUDENT ACTIVITIES
The school shall be responsible for the maintenance of the school discipline
among the students inside the school campus as well as outside the school
premises whenever they are engaged in authorized school activities. Classrooms
are to be used for academic purposes only. No group actions like boycott of
classes/demonstrations against any issue, person or teacher will be allowed.
Co-curricular activity shall be subjected to prior approval from the school
head. Meeting/assemblies, convocation and activities shall be held in the presence
of and with the knowledge of the principal, guidance counselor, teachers, or club
advisers/coaches.
4.1 SUPREME STUDENT AFFAIRS ORGANIZATION/
PUPIL AFFAIRS ORGANIZATION
The Supreme Student Affairs Organizations (SSAO)/ Pupil Affairs
Organization (PAO) is one of the two highest student/ pupil organizations in the
11
campus. Its role is to give support to the school in all scholastics, sports, spiritual
and other co and extracurricular activities
4.2 CAMPUS MINISTRY ORGANIZATION
This is the other student/pupil organization of highest authority in the school.
Its role is to support the school in all its religious activities.
CLUBS
A student/pupil may belong to no more than two (2) clubs. Membership in
more than the advised number is strongly discouraged for this may adversely affect
his/her academic performance.
A student who is active in school activities is expected to maintain a good
scholastic record. If these activities seem to be the cause of poor academic
performance, he/she will be required to cease his/her participation.
All clubs/councils/organizations are required to submit to the SSAO or CMO
coordinators the list of members and officers, constitution and by-laws and plan of
action for the school year for endorsement and approval of the principal before the
induction ceremony so that they will be recognized as official clubs/councils and
organizations of the school.
Clubs may collect membership fees approved by the Principal and VP for
Student Ministry. The amount collected from the membership should be turned
over to the cashier’s office. All clubs are required to submit financial statements at
the end of the school year. This should be signed by the club president, the
treasurer and the supervising adviser.
No students’ organization may impose upon its member any fine, penalty or
punishment of any kind except those provided for in its constitution and by-laws
All activities undertaken by the recognized club should have the permit from the
administration at least one week before the schedule.
Any damage on the materials, equipment borrowed will be charged to the
person or club concerned. The cleanliness of the venue used should be maintained
during and after the activity.
For weekend practices/rehearsal/activities, except Sunday, a written letter
from the adviser(s) should be made and submitted to the office of the principal for
approval indicating the names of the students who will be involved with the copy
furnished to the guard on duty. Concerned students must bring their ID, waiver or
parent’s consent signed by their parents/guardians.
4.3 INTRAMURAL
Various intramural competitions are organized within the school. Those
students/pupils who are not actively engaged in a particular event are expected to
give support to their team. Participants must wear the appropriate intramural
uniforms. All students are expected to display a spirit of true sportsmanship in
these competitions.
4.4 EXTRAMURAL
To help develop positive habits and attitudes among students/pupils, the
school participates in outside athletic and cultural events. Provided they are
maintaining appropriate levels of academic achievement, students/pupils may be
selected for membership of such team.
4.5 CAMPING, FIELD TRIPS AND RELIGIOUS ACTIVITIES
Various classes need to undertake such out-of-school trips and excursions to
adequately fulfill their curriculum requirements. The subject teacher/coordinator
will obtain the approval of the principal two weeks before the event. Students/
12
pupils will supply their parents with all details of the trip and obtain their written
consent at least 1 week before the event.
5 STUDENT CONDUCT AND BEHAVIOR
5.1 FLAG CEREMONY
The school day begins with the morning assembly and flag ceremony at 6:50
a.m. All students must attend this ceremony punctually. At the first bell, all students
move to the quadrangle and form their lines. The Class Beadle/Adviser checks the
attendance.
Every student should show the value of nationalism by singing the National
Anthem and reciting the Pledge of Allegiance properly. At the end of the flag
ceremony, students should go to their respective rooms quietly.
5.2 IN THE CLASSROOM AND DURING CLASSES
Classrooms should be kept clean and orderly at all times. Every student
should always be ready with the necessary materials needed for every classroom
activity. Students should respect the rights and properties of their classmates and
teachers. Acts of vandalism are strictly prohibited. Silence should be observed
during or in between classes and in the absence of the teacher. Avoid boisterous
laughter, shout, whistle and other unnecessary noise. Eating is not allowed during
class hours.
Pay respect to the teachers, superiors or visitors who enter or leave the
classroom. Every student should be participative in all classroom activities.
5.3 IN THE WORKSHOP, COMPUTER ROOM, TLE ROOM
The workshop, computer room and TLE room are places solely for work and
hands-on activities. Always observe silence. Take proper care of the tools,
computer units and paraphernalia. Each student has his/her own materials ready
and use them for his/her activities when required. Return all borrowed tools to
the subject teacher or to the property in-charge. Clean the workshop, computer
room and TLE room after using and before leaving it. Eating is strictly prohibited in
these areas.
5.4 INSIDE THE CAMPUS
The school is an environment-friendly institution; hence, cleanliness and
orderliness must be observed. Pay respect to classmates, schoolmates, teachers
and all school authorities by avoiding unnecessary noise that may cause
annoyance and distractions. Students are to refrain from sitting in parapets or
windowsills and stairs. Moreover, students are not allowed to wear caps inside the
school, office, in the chapel and along the corridor. Students should not stay inside
the school campus beyond curfew hours unless they are engaged in school
activities with permits granted by the principal.
Curfew Time: Curfew time is at 6:00 P.M. during class days.
5.5 DURING SCHOOL ACTIVITIES
Before the activity, students should attend to their personal needs and be
properly seated before the start of the program.
During the activity, observe silence and pay attention to the talk, ceremony or
performance. Applaud with responsible enthusiasm at the appropriate time.
Refrain from loud laughter and boisterous sound.
After the activity, everyone should leave the venue quietly and in an orderly
manner.
For safety and security, spearheading organizations must conduct an
13
orientation before the start of the activity.
5.6 USE OF DOOR PASS
Students must stay inside their rooms during class hours. However, for
personal necessity or any valid reason, only one-two students are permitted to go
out of the classroom using a door pass issued by the high school principal.
5.7 USE OF VEHICLE PASS
A vehicle pass is issued to all students/pupils who come to school in their
own transport. Vehicle owners must secure a vehicle pass, otherwise they are not
allowed to enter the school campus.
5.8 IN THE SCHOOL CANTEEN
The school canteen is available for snacks and lunch. However,
students/pupils must not go to the canteen except on break times. Students/pupils
need to line up in an orderly manner. Students/pupils must always keep the
canteen neat and tidy. They must clean the table, arrange the chairs before leaving
and dispose of trash in the bins provided.
5.9 IN THE CLINIC
The clinic nurse is on duty every school day. Clinic hours are 7:00 – 12:00
NN. and 1:00 – 5:00 PM. Arrangements to see the doctor who comes twice a week
are to be made through the clinic nurse.
5.10 DURING OFF CAMPUS ACTIVITIES
Students must behave in the utmost way possible. They should
conscientiously abide with the instructions of the teacher in–charge/
chaperone/coaches, observe the prescribed rules and regulations of the host
school/organizers, and always wear their “Visitor’s Pass/ID” properly.
5.11 CARE OF SCHOOL PROPERTY
Interest and pride in the school should prompt KCC students/pupils to regard
the school property with care and to keep the buildings and the campus neat and
clean. Any damage done by the student/pupil to the school property or of others
shall be repaired at the student’s expense. The classroom is a place for study and
its facilities should be used properly. Outside class hours, students/pupils who wish
to engage in serious study and quiet preparation for class should use the library. If
they have to leave their bags or other items inside the room, students/pupils must
secure their valuables. Bags, books or other items are not allowed on the benches
outside the rooms and in the corridors.
6 PARENTS’ / GUARDIANS’ SUPPORT AND CONDUCT IN SCHOOL
The work of education for all the students/pupils of Kabankalan Catholic
College, Inc. requires the active cooperation of the parents/guardians of the
student/pupils.
Daily concern for the academic progress of students/ pupils is an important
duty of parents/guardians. This includes general interest in their daily learning at
school as well as helping them attend to out-of-class activities such as homework,
subject research, acquiring correct textbooks, etc.
During the school year, there are many activities involving the students/pupils
to which parents/guardians and family are cordially invited. These would include a
range of religious, cultural and sporting events. Students/pupils certainly derive
great encouragement from the presence of the parents and friends on such
occasions.
The other side of parent involvement is unfortunately, the negative one where
14
students/pupils become involved in incorrect behavior. Minor infringements of
school rules and discipline are usually dealt with by the teachers and/or other staff
members. More serious breaches of discipline need to be dealt with by the
Disciplinary Committee. Parents will be invited to attend the hearings of the
committee together with their son/daughter and to speak with the appropriate
school officials, e.g. the Classroom Adviser, the Discipline Officer, the Principal,
about any misconduct of their student/pupil and the appropriate penalty that the
school Principal imposes on the student/pupil. This may include reparation for any
damage done.
Parents who come to school to set an appointment with a teacher or a student
can only be done through the Principal’s Office.
A parent who comes to school to pay fees should directly go to the Cashier’s
Office.
Parents who come to school should observe the school’s dress code.
