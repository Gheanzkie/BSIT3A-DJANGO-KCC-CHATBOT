# KCC Campus Life - Blessing of New Buildings (CEAS and CMAT)
Blessing of the New CEAS Building (January 16, 2026): A two-storey academic facility featuring an integrated swimming pool - a testament to KCC's commitment to academic excellence, holistic formation, and continuous enhancement of learning environments. The blessing ceremony was presided over by Most Rev. Louie P. Galbines, SThD, DD, together with the KCC administrators and faculty.
Blessing of the New CMAT Building: The college celebrated the blessing of its new building for the College of Management, Accountancy, and Technology (CMAT) - a renewed commitment to excellence in education and service. "All Hail, KCC! To God be the glory!"
