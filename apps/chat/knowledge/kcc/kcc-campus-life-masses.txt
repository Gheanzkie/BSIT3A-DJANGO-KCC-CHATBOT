# KCC Campus Life - Spiritual Activities and Masses
Holy Spirit Mass: Theme "Come Holy Spirit, Lead Us, Pilgrims of Hope", held at the St. Columban Arts Center - a meaningful start of the academic year; the event included the introduction of the faculty, staff, and personnel of the institution.
Nativity of Mama Mary: Morning Mass led by Rev. Fr. Freddie Delatado with the Elementary and High School departments; afternoon Mass celebrated by School President Rev. Fr. Richard Lachica with the College Department.
Holy Mass - 11th Educators Congress: Led by Rev. Fr. Emmanuel G. Magno, PhL, together with the parochial schools of the Diocese of Kabankalan.
