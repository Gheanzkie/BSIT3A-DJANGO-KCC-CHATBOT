# KCC Campus Life - Community and Outreach Programs
Dinalagan 2025: The KCC Counseling and Testing Office spearheaded "DINALAGAN 2025: A Run for Mental Health Awareness" on October 11, 2025, promoting mental health awareness and overall well-being within the community. Participants ran not just for fitness but for a cause - to remind everyone that mental health truly matters.
Blood Donation Activity: Held in connection with the 129th Philippine Army Founding Anniversary.
Christmas Community Outreach: The KCC community organized a Christmas gift-giving and feeding program for underprivileged families in Kabankalan City - food packs, gift bags, and educational materials, with a prayer service and fellowship. It reflects KCC's core value of Kindness and its preferential option for the poor.
