# KCC History - Foundation (1927-1953)
1927 - Founding as Kabankalan Institute: In 1927 a group of incorporators gathered to establish a secondary school named Kabankalan Institute which was later changed to Kabankalan Private Academy.
1940-1946 - First Commencement & The War Years: The first commencement exercises of the school was in 1940. Classes were suspended during the Second World War (1941-1944) and were resumed in school year 1945-1946. The Board of Trustees got a permit to open a Junior Normal College which lasted only three years due to poor enrollment.
1953 - Columban Fathers Acquire the Academy: In 1953, the Columban Fathers through Fr. Thomas J. Cronin, parish priest of Kabankalan, thought of putting up a new school but later negotiated to buy Kabankalan Academy. Most stockholders donated their shares in exchange for scholarship privileges.
