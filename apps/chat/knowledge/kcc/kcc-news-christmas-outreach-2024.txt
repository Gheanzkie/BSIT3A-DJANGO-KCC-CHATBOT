KCC Community Outreach: Christmas Gift-Giving and Feeding Program
Date: December 2024 | Category: Community
In keeping with its core value of Kindness and its mission as a preferential option for the poor, the KCC community organized a Christmas gift-giving and feeding program for underprivileged families in Kabankalan City.
Students, faculty, and staff came together to prepare food packs, gift bags, and educational materials for distribution. The outreach program was accompanied by a prayer service and fellowship activities.
The initiative reflects KCC's commitment to community service and its role as an evangelizing arm of the Diocese of Kabankalan.
