# KCC Announces Expanded Programs for College Department
Date: December 2024 | Category: Announcement
Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. continues to grow its academic offerings in the College Department, expanding opportunities for students in Kabankalan City and surrounding areas.
The institution remains committed to providing quality and inclusive Catholic education, as stated in its mission, empowering its members to become effective agents of societal transformation.
Students interested in the new and existing programs are encouraged to visit the admissions office or contact the school for more information.
