KCC Basic Education - Elementary (K-6)
- Strong Academic Foundation: A comprehensive K-6 curriculum aligned with DepEd standards, integrating Catholic values in every subject area.
- Faith Formation: Regular liturgical celebrations, prayer services, and Christian Living classes that nurture spiritual growth from an early age.
- Arts & Creativity: Programs in visual arts, music, and performing arts that develop creative expression and artistic appreciation.
- Math & Science: Engaging instruction using hands-on activities and inquiry-based learning approaches.
- Language Proficiency: Strong emphasis on English and Filipino communication skills, with reading enrichment programs.
- Character Development: Values education integrated across all learning areas, building responsible and compassionate young learners.
