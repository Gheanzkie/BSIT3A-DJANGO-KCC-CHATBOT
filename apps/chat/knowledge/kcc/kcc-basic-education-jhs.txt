KCC Basic Education - Junior High School (Grades 7-10)
- Holistic Curriculum: A well-rounded JHS program following the K-12 Enhanced Basic Education curriculum with Catholic integration.
- STEM Readiness: Strong foundation in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics to prepare students for SHS tracks.
- Research & Innovation: Introduction to basic research methodologies, encouraging curiosity and scientific inquiry.
- Co-Curricular Activities: Scouting, campus journalism, sports, quiz bees, and competitions at division and regional levels.
- Spiritual Programs: Retreats, recollections, masses, and community outreach that form students in Christian service and values.
- Student Leadership: Supreme Student Government and class organizations.
Subjects/learning areas: Araling Panlipunan, MAPEH (Music, Arts, PE, Health), Edukasyon sa Pagpapakatao (EsP), Technology and Livelihood Education (TLE), Christian Living / Religious Education.
Why choose KCC Basic Education: DepEd-aligned K-12 curriculum; small class sizes; dedicated and experienced faculty; safe and nurturing campus; regular spiritual formation; co-curricular and extracurricular programs; parent involvement.
