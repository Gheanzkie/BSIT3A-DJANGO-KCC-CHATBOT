Elementary Department Conducts 57th Graduation & Moving Up Ceremonies
Date: March 2025 | Category: Graduation
The Kabankalan Catholic College Elementary Department held its 57th Graduation and Moving Up Ceremonies, marking a significant milestone for the young KCCians.
The event celebrated the completion of elementary education and recognized outstanding student achievements in academics, co-curricular activities, and values formation.
Parents and guardians joined the celebration as their children received their diplomas and awards, prepared to continue their academic journey in junior high school.
