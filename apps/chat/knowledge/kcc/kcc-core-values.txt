# KCC Core Values (7)
Pitong (7) core values ng KCC at ang kahulugan ng bawat isa:

1. Kindness - A KCCian upholds a deep sense of unity and empathy, embodying shared purpose and mutual support within the school and wider community.
2. Christ-Centeredness - A KCCian is grounded in the living faith in God, guided by Gospel values that inspire words and actions in everyday life.
3. Creativity - A KCCian embraces innovation in the pursuit of new ideas through research and creative problem-solving for the service of Church and society.
4. Integrity - A KCCian is committed to moral responsibility and academic honesty, exemplifying Gospel-rooted principles in academic works, personal choices, and leadership.
5. Accountability - A KCCian takes ownership of his/her decisions and actions, contributing responsibly to society and upholding the standards of a globally competent individual.
6. Nurturing - A KCCian cares for God's creation as responsible steward entrusted to protect, sustain, and honor the world He has made.
7. Synodality - A KCCian journeys together with others in communion, fostering collaboration and active participation in the mission of the Church and community.
