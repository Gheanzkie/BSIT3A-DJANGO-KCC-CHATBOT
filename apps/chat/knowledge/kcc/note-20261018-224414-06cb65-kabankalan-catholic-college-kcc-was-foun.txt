Kabankalan Catholic College (KCC) was founded in 1927 as Kabankalan Institute, later known as Kabankalan Private Academy. Its first commencement exercises were held in 1940, but classes were suspended from 1941 to 1944 due to World War II and resumed in School Year 1945–1946. In 1953, the Columban Fathers, led by Fr. Thomas J. Cronin, acquired Kabankalan Academy. During the 1963–1964 school year, the College and Elementary departments opened with assistance from the St. Paul Sisters. In 1983, diocesan administration began under Msgr. Josefino I. Iledan, with support from the Presentation Sisters. In 1987, a great fire destroyed the high school building, parish convent, and Old Catholic church, leading to reconstruction efforts. The following year, in 1988, Kabankalan became a new diocese under its first bishop, Most Rev. Vicente M. Navarra, while the Good Shepherd Sisters served the school for a year. Campus development continued with the establishment of the St. Columban Arts Center and additional facilities, including a canteen and library.
