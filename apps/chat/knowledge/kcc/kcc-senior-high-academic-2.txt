KCC Senior High - Academic Track Strands (HUMSS and GAS)
HUMSS (Humanities and Social Sciences): For students who wish to take up journalism, communication arts, liberal arts, education, and other social science programs in college. Key subjects: Creative Writing, Creative Nonfiction, Introduction to World Religions, Trends/Networks/Critical Thinking, Philippine Politics & Governance, Community Engagement. College paths: Education, Psychology, Mass Communication, Public Administration.
GAS (General Academic Strand): A flexible strand for students who are still undecided on their college course; covers a broad range of subjects from different academic tracks. Key subjects: Humanities, Social Sciences, Electives from other strands. College paths: Any college program, Liberal Arts, Business.
