KCC School Seal - Meaning and Symbols
The Twelve Waves: The twelve (12) waves at the rim of the logo represent the twelve (12) apostles tasked by Christ to teach and evangelize.
The Motto: The school motto "TEACH YE ALL NATIONS" expresses KCC's untiring response to Christ's call for evangelization. Every KCCian should strive to be a model Christian and true patriot.
The Two Stars: Symbolize the guiding light of Catholic Education. One star represents KCC's identity, rooted in Catholic faith and values; the other its mission to spread knowledge, truth, and service to others.
The Philippine Map: The island of Negros represents the immediate world KCC aims to evangelize; the other islands represent the world KCC hopes to reach out to.
Dark Blue & Gold: Symbolizes wisdom, integrity, and excellence. Dark blue = knowledge and moral strength; gold = the eternal light of Christ guiding KCCians to success and dignity.
