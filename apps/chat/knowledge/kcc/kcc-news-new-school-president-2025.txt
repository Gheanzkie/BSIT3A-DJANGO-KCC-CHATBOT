Fr. Richard L. Lachica, MAEd Appointed as New KCC School President
Date: April 15, 2025 | Category: Announcement
Most Rev. Louie P. Galbines, SThD, D.D., Bishop of the Diocese of Kabankalan and Chairman of the KCC Board of Trustees, has officially appointed Rev. Fr. Richard L. Lachica, MAEd, as the new school president of Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc., effective Academic Year 2025-2026.
Fr. Lachica takes over the leadership from Rev. Fr. Eugene D. Lucerna II, PhD, RPsy, RGC, who served as school president since 2019 and was the first alumnus to hold the position.
The KCC community warmly welcomes Fr. Lachica and looks forward to his leadership in continuing the institution's mission of providing quality Catholic education in Kabankalan City.
