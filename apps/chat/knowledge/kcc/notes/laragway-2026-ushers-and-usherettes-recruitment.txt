Laragway 2026 Ushers and Usherettes Recruitment
Laragway 2026 is recruiting 4 ushers and 2 usherettes for the upcoming Talent Night and Coronation Night. Candidates must be willing to undergo training, attend rehearsals, and be available during both events. They should be courteous, approachable, respectful, responsible, punctual, committed, presentable, and able to communicate well with guests. Applicants should be willing to work as part of a team. Interested applicants should send a direct message to the official Laragway page or to the Facebook account of Reynald Maglasang‑Laragway Coordinator.
