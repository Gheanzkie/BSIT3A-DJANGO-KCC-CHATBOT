KCC ROTC Unit 12th Week of Instruction
IMAGE: knowledge/photos/20261113121546-41b898ea.png
The KCC ROTC Unit conducted its 12th Week of Instruction for the First Semester of A.Y. 2026–2027 on November 7, 2026, focusing on Drills and Ceremonies. The activity aimed to enhance cadets’ military discipline, coordination, confidence, and proper execution of military movements and commands. Under the guidance of Sgt Elviro E Apawan Jr PA (Res), ROTC Coordinator, the cadets practiced formations, movements, and command execution, emphasizing discipline, precision, teamwork, and proper military bearing. The cadets strengthened coordination, confidence, and unit performance, reinforcing values of discipline, respect, responsibility, and unity. The KCC ROTC Unit thanks the KCC Administration, headed by Rev. Fr. Richard L. Lachica, MAEd, KCC President; Jomar O. Agus, MAT, NSTP Director; CPT Jose S.
