Calling All KCC Rocc Cadets
All KCC Rocc Cadets are invited to the 13th Week of Instruction, 1st Semester, A.Y 2026-2027 (Half Day) at Kabankalan Catholic College Inc. (KCC). The event starts earlier than 0700H on 14 November 2026 (07:00 A.M). Cadets should wear a Type C uniform: plain white round neck T‑shirt, denim/maong pants, black or dominant black athletic shoes, white socks, and a garison belt black with white buckle. Strict reminders: no accessories or garison belt with silver buckle; follow the standard haircut (2×3), neat and well groomed hair, and trimmed beards and mustache; bun hair must use gel/hair net; no hair dye exceeding color spectrums 7 and nails must be cut. Cadets are guided by the KCC Rocc page; for concerns, message the page or follow it for more information.
