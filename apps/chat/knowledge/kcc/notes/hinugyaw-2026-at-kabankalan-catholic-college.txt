Hinugyaw 2026 at Kabankalan Catholic College
Hinugyaw 2026 was held in September 2026 with opening ceremonies and house competitions that concluded in mid‑to‑late September. The theme was “Ad Saeculum et Ultra: Breaking Limits through a Century of Courage, Excellence, Faith, and Love.” The overall champion house was Domus Virtutis with 3,055 points, followed by Domus Excelsa, Domus Fidei, and Domus Caritatis. Four main house factions—Domus Virtutis, Domus Excelsa, Domus Fidei, and Domus Caritatis—competed in sports, bench cheering, and cultural showcases.
