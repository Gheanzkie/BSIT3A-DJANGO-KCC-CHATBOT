KCC Registrar's Office Services
The Registrar's Office manages student academic records and related documentation. Common services include Transcript of Records, Certificate of Grades, Certificate of Enrollment, Certificate of Good Moral Character, Honorable Dismissal, Diploma requests, student record corrections, academic evaluation, enrollment verification, and student credentials. For a Transcript of Records, students should contact the Registrar's Office and inquire about the application form, required documents, clearance requirements, processing fees, and expected release date. Certificate of Enrollment requests are also handled by the Registrar's Office; the exact requirements, fees, and processing time are subject to official confirmation.
