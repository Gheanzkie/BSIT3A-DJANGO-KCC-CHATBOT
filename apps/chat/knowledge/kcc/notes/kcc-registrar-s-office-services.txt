# KCC REGISTRAR'S OFFICE SERVICES



The Registrar's Office handles student academic records and related school documentation.



## Common Registrar Transactions



1. Transcript of Records (TOR)

2. Certificate of Grades

3. Certificate of Enrollment

4. Certificate of Good Moral Character

5. Honorable Dismissal

6. Diploma requests

7. Student record corrections

8. Academic evaluation

9. Enrollment verification

10. Student credentials



## TOR Request



Students requesting a Transcript of Records should coordinate with the Registrar's Office.



They should ask about:

- TOR application form.

- Required documents.

- Clearance requirements.

- Processing fees.

- Expected release date.



## Certificate of Enrollment



Students should request a Certificate of Enrollment through the Registrar's Office.



The exact requirements, fees, and processing time are subject to official confirmation.
