# Mga naging presidente ng KCC

Pangalan	Panahon / detalye

Msgr. Josefino I. Iledan	Naging College President noong panahon ng pamamahala ng mga diocesan priest, simula 1983.

Msgr. Rogelio B. Cruz	College President noong 1996, nang tumulong ang Carmelite Sisters of Our Lady sa pamamahala ng paaralan.

Most Rev. Patricio A. Buzon, SDB, D.D.	Naging School President at Chairman of the Board of Trustees noong 2005.

Rev. Fr. Radny T. Bayog, Ph.D.	Itinalagang School President noong 2011.

Rev. Fr. Eugene D. Lucerna II	Nagsimulang manungkulan noong School Year 2019–2020 at nagsilbi nang humigit-kumulang anim na taon.

Rev. Fr. Richard L. Lachica, MAEd	Itinalagang bagong School President noong Abril 15, 2025, para sa AY 2025–2026.

Note: Rev. Fr. Eugene D. Lucerna II was the first alumnus president; Rev. Fr. Richard L. Lachica was installed as the 17th school president on July 11, 2025.
