KCC Days at Kabankalan Catholic College
KCC Days are annual milestone celebrations marking the institution’s founding anniversary. The event commemorates 99 years of Catholic education as of the current academic year. The celebration opens with holy masses, faith formation, and liturgies such as the First Friday/Holy Spirit Masses and First Communion rites held at venues like the St. Columban Arts Center or the local cathedral. Student competitions include the Hinugyaw Opening Program, the Udyakan Dance Arena Competition, and Laragway, a pageant for Mr. and Ms. KCC featuring sashing ceremonies, talent nights, and a grand coronation night. Sports and gaming championships feature Mobile Legends tournaments and athletic games. Elementary and basic education departments host campus booths and playground demonstrations, while student organizations lead community outreach programs, medical and dental missions, and other outreach activities.
