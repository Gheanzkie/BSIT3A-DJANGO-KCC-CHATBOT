KCC Knowledge Base Import Procedure
All files in the “kcc” folder were imported from kcc.edu.ph: Home, About, Programs, Basic Education, Senior High, Admissions, Campus Life, News, Contact, and Handbook pages. Facts were taken from public site pages and content data such as news articles and program records. The full raw dump is stored in knowledge/_kcc-full-site-dump-20261017.txt and is ignored by the loader. When the school publishes changes (new president, programs, contacts, news), edit or add files in the kcc folder; they will be picked up automatically on the next query. The source of truth is kcc.edu.ph. Oct 17 2026 updates include master lists (courses, SHS strands, history, news, campus life), a colleges/departments file, and an FAQ covering tuition, teachers, and scholarships. Natural/descriptive phrasing is used; data language is in the knowledge folder.
