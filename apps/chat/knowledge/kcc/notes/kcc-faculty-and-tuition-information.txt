KCC Faculty and Tuition Information
The KCC website lists all teachers and faculty. For faculty concerns contact the Registrar's Office or school administration. Dean office notes are available for dean office information. Tuition and fee schedules are not published on the website; contact the Registrar's Office or Accounting Office at (032) 4712‑479 or kcc_1927@yahoo.com for actual tuition and fees. Scholarships and financial assistance are offered to qualifying students; inquire at the Registrar's Office.
