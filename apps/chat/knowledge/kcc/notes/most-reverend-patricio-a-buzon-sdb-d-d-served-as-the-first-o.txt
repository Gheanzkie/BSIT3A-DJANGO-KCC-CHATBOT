Most Reverend Patricio A. Buzon, SDB, D.D. served as the first official school president when the administration structured the school under a presidential leadership model in 2005 (while also acting as Chairman of the Board of Trustees).
Prior to that, the institution was founded in 1927 as the Kabankalan Institute (later renamed Kabankalan Private Academy), and was managed and directed by parish priests—most notably Fr. Thomas J. Cronin of the Columban Fathers who acquired the academy in 1953 and opened the college department in 1963–1964—rather than a designated school president.
