KCC Mobile App and Digital Information
KCC has a mobile application available on Google Play, developed by LIVRO SYSTEMS INC. The app offers student and parent announcements, school news and updates, a calendar of school activities, mobile verification code authentication, and organized school information. The Google Play listing is https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wela.kcc. Official online channels include the website https://kcc.edu.ph/, the Facebook page https://www.facebook.com/KCCInc1927, and the email address kcc_1927@yahoo.com.
