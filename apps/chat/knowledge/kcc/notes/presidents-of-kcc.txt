Presidents of KCC
Msgr. Josefino I. Iledan served as College President from 1983 during the diocesan priest administration.
Msgr. Rogelio B. Cruz was College President in 1996 when the Carmelite Sisters of Our Lady assisted in school management.
Most Rev. Patricio A. Buzon, SDB, D.D. was School President and Chairman of the Board of Trustees in 2005.
Rev. Fr. Radny T. Bayog, Ph.D. was appointed School President in 2011.
Rev. Fr. Eugene D. Lucerna II began his term in the School Year 2019–2020 and served for approximately six years.
Rev. Fr. Richard L. Lachica, MAEd was appointed new School President on April 15, 2025, for AY 2025–2026.
Rev. Fr. Eugene D. Lucerna II was the first alumnus president; Rev. Fr. Richard L. Lachica was installed as the 17th school president on July 11, 2025.
