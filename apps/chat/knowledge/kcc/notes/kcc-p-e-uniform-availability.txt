KCC P.E. Uniform Availability
The KCC announcement dated August 20, 2026 says P.E. uniforms can be claimed at the Economic Enterprise Office beside the School Canteen. Students must present the official receipt. XL‑size pants were temporarily unavailable at the time of the announcement. The announcement reflects availability at the time it was published, so students should confirm current stock, prices, sizes, and claiming schedules before visiting.
