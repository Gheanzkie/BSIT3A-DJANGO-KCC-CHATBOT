# KCC SCHOOL OFFICES AND SERVICES



## 1. Registrar's Office

Handles:

- Student enrollment records.

- Academic documents.

- Transcript requests.

- Certificates.

- Student academic information.



## 2. Accounting Office

Handles:

- Tuition assessment.

- School fee payments.

- Financial transactions.

- Payment verification.

- Outstanding balances.



## 3. Economic Enterprise Office

A KCC announcement identified this office as the location for P.E. uniform claiming. It was formerly referred to as the Accounting Office in that announcement.



Location mentioned: Beside the School Canteen.



## 4. Student Affairs and Services Office (SSAO)

Handles student affairs and student-related services. Exact service procedures should be confirmed with the office.



## 5. College of Criminology

Handles program-related academic activities and student concerns.
