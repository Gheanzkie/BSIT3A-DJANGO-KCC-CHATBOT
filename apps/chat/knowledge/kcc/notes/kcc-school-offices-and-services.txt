KCC School Offices and Services
The Registrar’s Office manages student enrollment records, academic documents, transcript requests, certificates, and student academic information.
The Accounting Office handles tuition assessment, school fee payments, financial transactions, payment verification, and outstanding balances.
The Economic Enterprise Office, formerly referred to as the Accounting Office in a KCC announcement, is the location for P.E. uniform claiming and is situated beside the School Canteen.
The Student Affairs and Services Office (SSAO) handles student affairs and student‑related services; exact procedures should be confirmed with the office.
The College of Criminology handles program‑related academic activities and student concerns.
