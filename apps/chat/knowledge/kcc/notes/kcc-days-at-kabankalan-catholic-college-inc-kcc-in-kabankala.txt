KCC Days at Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. (KCC) in Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental are annual milestone celebrations marking the institution's founding anniversary.

# Key Highlights and Traditions

- Anniversary Milestone: KCC celebrates nearly a century of Catholic education (marking 99 years of excellence as of the current academic year).

- Spiritual Activities: The celebration opens and centers around holy masses, faith formation, and liturgies (such as the First Friday/Holy Spirit Masses and First Communion rites) held at venues like the St. Columban Arts Center or local cathedral.

- Student Competitions & Events:

- Hinugyaw Opening Program: High-energy dance and spirit cheers.

- Udyakan Dance Arena Competition

- Laragway: The highly anticipated search/pageant for Mr. and Ms. KCC featuring sashing ceremonies, talent nights, and grand coronation nights.

- Sports & Gaming Championships: Mobile Legends (ML) tournaments and athletic games.

- Campus Booths & Playground Demonstrations by the elementary and basic education departments.

- Community Outreach: Extension service programs, medical/dental missions, and outreach activities led by student organizations
