KCC Celebrates 98th Foundation Day
Date: February 2025 | Category: Event
Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. celebrated its 98th Foundation Day with a week-long series of activities, including a thanksgiving mass, academic competitions, cultural presentations, sports events, and community outreach.
Founded in 1927, KCC has grown from a small secondary school called Kabankalan Institute to a full-fledged educational institution offering programs from elementary to college. The Foundation Day celebrations highlight KCC's rich history and its continuing commitment to the mission of "Teaching Ye All Nations."
The celebration also featured an alumni homecoming, where former KCCians reconnected with their alma mater and shared their success stories.
