KCC History - School Presidents (2003-2019)
2003-2011 - Bishop Buzon's Era: Most Reverend Patricio A. Buzon, SDB, D.D. was installed as the second Bishop of the Diocese of Kabankalan in 2003. In 2005 he administered the School as Chairman of the Board of Trustees and as School President. Under his management, the KCC-Technological Center in Barangay Talubangi, Kabankalan City was established in 2009. In 2011, Rev. Fr. Radny T. Bayog, PhD was appointed as School President of KCC.
2018-2019 - Bishop Galbines & First Alumnus President: In 2018, Most Rev. Louie P. Galbines, SThD, D.D. was installed as the third Bishop of the Diocese of Kabankalan and Chairman of the school's Board of Trustees. In 2019, Rev. Fr. Eugene D. Lucerna II, PhD, RPsy, RGC was appointed as School President - the first alumnus (KCC High School Batch 1996) to hold the position.
