# KCC Senior High School - Complete Strand List (Academic + TVL)
Lahat ng SHS tracks at strands ng KCC (7):
Academic Track (for college-bound students): 1. STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics); 2. ABM (Accountancy, Business and Management); 3. HUMSS (Humanities and Social Sciences); 4. GAS (General Academic Strand).
TVL - Technical Professional Track (hands-on skills): 5. ICT (Information and Communications Technology); 6. Home Economics; 7. Industrial Arts.
Every track includes Catholic values formation. After SHS: college, employment, or entrepreneurship.
