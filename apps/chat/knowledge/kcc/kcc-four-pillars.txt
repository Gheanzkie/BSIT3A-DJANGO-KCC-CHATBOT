# KCC Four Pillars
I. Academic Excellence - Rigorous academics that challenge students to think critically and develop a genuine love for learning.
II. Faith Formation - Deepening faith through Catholic tradition, prayer, and a personal relationship with God.
III. Community Service - Reaching out to those in need, putting the Gospel into practice through hands-on service.
IV. Holistic Development - Shaping students who are not only skilled and knowledgeable but also grounded in strong values.
