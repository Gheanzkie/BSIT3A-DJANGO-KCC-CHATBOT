KCC Senior High School Overview (Grades 11-12)
KCC Senior High School has both Academic and TVL (Technical-Vocational-Livelihood) tracks so students can follow the path that fits their interests and goals. Every track includes Catholic values formation and activities that develop the whole person.
- Academic Track: for students headed to college or university. Strands: STEM, ABM, HUMSS, GAS.
- Technical Professional Track (TVL): for students who want hands-on skills for work, business, or further technical training. Strands: ICT, Home Economics, Industrial Arts.
After Senior High: college, employment, or entrepreneurship. "Not sure which strand is right for you? Our guidance counselors can help you figure it out." (Options: Apply for SHS / Talk to a Counselor)
