# KCC History - Columban Era and College Opening (1963)
Columban Era - Transfer to the Church Site: When the Columban Fathers took over, the first decision was to transfer the site of the school to the space near the church. The school directorship under the Columban Fathers was not a permanent position; the change of directorship went with the reshuffling of the parish priest.
1963-1964 - College & Elementary Departments Open: Through the untiring efforts of Fr. Cronin, a general college and elementary education was opened in school year 1963-1964. Two years later, he secured the services of the St. Paul Sisters of Chartres to assist in the management of the school. Through them, Kabankalan College became the center of learning in the southern part of Negros Occidental.
