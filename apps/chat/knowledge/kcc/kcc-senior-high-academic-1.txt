KCC Senior High - Academic Track Strands (STEM and ABM)
STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics): For students who intend to pursue college degrees in the sciences, engineering, technology, mathematics, and related fields. Key subjects: Pre-Calculus, Basic Calculus, General Biology, General Chemistry, General Physics, Research/Capstone. College paths: Engineering, Medicine, IT/Computer Science, Architecture, Research.
ABM (Accountancy, Business, and Management): Designed for students who plan to pursue business-related courses in college such as business administration, accountancy, marketing, and entrepreneurship. Key subjects: Fundamentals of ABM, Business Mathematics, Business Finance, Organization & Management, Applied Economics, Principles of Marketing. College paths: Accountancy, Business Administration, Entrepreneurship, Marketing, Economics.
